## Repository Context & Terminology

- **Inventory**: The stock of goods or items held by a business.
- **Kafka**: A distributed streaming platform used for building real-time data pipelines and streaming applications.
- **Commit**: To permanently apply a change, in this case, reducing the inventory quantity.
- **Rollback**: To revert a change, in this case, restoring the inventory quantity to its previous state.
- **Deducting Quantity**: A temporary quantity that is reserved for deduction, before the deduction is finalized.
- **Insufficient Inventory**: A situation where the available inventory is less than the requested deduction quantity.
