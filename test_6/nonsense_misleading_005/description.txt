## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram depicting the order processing workflow of an e-commerce system. It illustrates the steps involved from when a user submits an order to when the order is delivered, including payment processing, inventory checks, and shipping. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the order fulfillment process.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) direction. It starts with the "User Submits Order" node at the top and flows downwards through a series of processes and decision points, ultimately ending with the "Order Delivered" node.
