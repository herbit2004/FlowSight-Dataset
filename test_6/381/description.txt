## Repository Context & Terminology
- **AI Governance Ecosystem**: A comprehensive framework encompassing standards, guidelines, regulations, and frameworks to govern the development and deployment of AI systems.
- **ISO/IEC Standards**: Internationally recognized standards developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC).
- **NIST AI RMF**: The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) AI Risk Management Framework, a resource to manage risks to individuals, organizations, and society associated with artificial intelligence (AI).
- **EU AI Act**: A proposed regulation by the European Commission on artificial intelligence.
- **GDPR**: The General Data Protection Regulation, a European Union regulation on data protection and privacy.
- **CCPA**: The California Consumer Privacy Act, a state statute intended to enhance privacy rights and consumer protection for California residents.
- **Data Minimization**: A principle that data controllers should limit the collection of personal data to what is directly relevant and necessary for a specified purpose.
- **Privacy by Design**: An approach to systems engineering which accounts for privacy throughout the entire engineering process.
