## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the data flow and dependencies within a customer data pipeline. It depicts how various data sources and transformations contribute to creating a 360-degree view of the customer, enabling risk assessment, lifetime value calculation, and customer segmentation. The diagram serves as a high-level overview of the data engineering processes involved in customer analytics.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) manner. It starts with staging data and progresses through various transformations to reach the final customer 360 view. The start nodes are `stg_customers`, `fact_orders`, and `fact_payments`, and the end node is `customer_360`.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **stg_customers**: Staging table for customer data, typically the initial landing zone for raw data extracted from source systems.
- **dim_customers**: Dimension table in a data warehouse, containing descriptive attributes of customers.
- **fact_orders**: Fact table recording customer order transactions.
- **fact_payments**: Fact table recording customer payment transactions.
- **at_risk_customers**: A segment of customers identified as having a high probability of churn.
- **churn_risk_score**: A numerical score representing the likelihood of a customer churning.
- **customer_lifetime_value**: A prediction of the net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer.
- **customer_segments**: Groups of customers with similar characteristics, used for targeted marketing and personalization.
- **customer_360**: A holistic view of the customer, aggregating data from various sources to provide a complete understanding of their interactions and attributes.

The `churn_risk_score` also contributes to the `customer_360` view. The final `customer_360` view integrates customer attributes, order history, payment information, risk scores, and segment assignments to provide a complete customer profile.
