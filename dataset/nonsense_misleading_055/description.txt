## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The order processing flowchart begins with "Start Order," immediately followed by a check: "Item in Stock?". If the item is not in stock ("No" branch), the customer is notified ("Notify Customer: Out of Stock"), and the order is then cancelled. If the item *is* in stock ("Yes" branch), the total is calculated ("Calculate Total"), and the system checks if payment has been received ("Payment Received?"). If payment *has* been received ("Yes" branch), the items are packaged ("Package Items"), a shipping label is printed ("Print Shipping Label"), and the order is shipped ("Ship Order"). If payment has *not* been received ("No" branch), the inventory is updated, skipping logical steps. After shipping, the system checks if delivery is confirmed ("Delivery Confirmed?"). If delivery is *not* confirmed ("No" branch), the delivery failure is investigated ("Investigate Delivery Failure"), and the process loops back to printing the shipping label. If delivery *is* confirmed ("Yes" branch), the inventory is updated. After updating the inventory, a confirmation email is sent ("Send Confirmation Email"), and the order is then cancelled. Finally, the "Cancel Order" node leads to "Order Completed". The "Delivery Confirmed?" node's "Yes" branch incorrectly triggers "Update Inventory" *after* shipping, contradicting standard inventory management practices where inventory is updated *before* fulfilling the order.
