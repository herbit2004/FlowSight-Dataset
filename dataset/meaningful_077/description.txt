## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the billing process within a system. It outlines the steps involved from receiving a bill request to completing the billing process, including validation, usage calculation, discount application, balance checks, bill generation, delivery, and system updates. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the billing workflow, aiding in understanding and potentially optimizing the process.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's layout is top-down (TD). It starts with the "Bill Request Received" node at the top and flows downwards through a series of processes and decision points, ultimately ending with the "Billing Process Complete" node. The diagram depicts a sequential process with branching based on validation results, billing cycles, outstanding balances, and delivery methods.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Bill request is sent for validation.
- `B -- Valid --> C`: If the request is valid, retrieve the customer account.
- `B -- Invalid --> Z`: If the request is invalid, reject it and log the error.
- `C --> D`: Determine the billing cycle after retrieving the customer account.
- `D -- Monthly --> E`: If the billing cycle is monthly, calculate monthly usage.
- `D -- Quarterly --> F`: If the billing cycle is quarterly, calculate quarterly usage.
- `E --> G`: Apply discounts and taxes after calculating monthly usage.
- `F --> G`: Apply discounts and taxes after calculating quarterly usage.
- `G --> H`: Check for any outstanding balance after applying discounts and taxes.
- `H -- Yes --> I`: If there is an outstanding balance, add it to the current bill.
- `H -- No --> J`: If there is no outstanding balance, continue to the next step.
- `I --> J`: Continue after adding the remaining balance.
- `J --> K`: Generate the bill summary after checking for outstanding balance.
- `K --> L`: Create a PDF bill after generating the bill summary.
- `L --> M`: Store the bill in the repository after creating the PDF bill.
- `M --> N`: Determine the delivery method after storing the bill.
- `N -- Email --> O`: If the delivery method is email, send an email notification.
- `N -- Postal --> P`: If the delivery method is postal, print and mail the bill.
- `N -- Online Portal --> Q`: If the delivery method is online portal, update the online portal.
- `O --> R`: Update the billing system after sending the email notification.
- `P --> R`: Update the billing system after printing and mailing the bill.
- `Q --> R`: Update the billing system after updating the online portal.
- `R --> S`: Mark the bill as generated after updating the billing system.
- `S --> T`: The billing process is complete after marking the bill as generated.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Bill Request**: A request to generate a bill for a customer, containing necessary information like account details and billing period.
- **Validation**: The process of verifying the accuracy and completeness of the bill request.
- **Billing Cycle**: The regular interval at which bills are generated (e.g., monthly, quarterly).
- **Usage Calculation**: The process of determining the amount of service or product consumed by the customer during the billing cycle.
- **Discounts and Taxes**: Reductions in price or additional charges applied to the bill based on various factors.
- **Outstanding Balance**: The amount of money owed by the customer from previous billing cycles.
- **Bill Summary**: A concise overview of the charges, discounts, and total amount due on the bill.
- **PDF Bill**: A digital version of the bill in PDF format.
- **Repository**: A storage location for bills, typically a database or file system.
- **Delivery Method**: The means by which the bill is delivered to the customer (e.g., email, postal mail, online portal).
