## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The `UserProfileService` initiates the purchase, triggering the `InventoryService` to verify stock.
- `B -- Insufficient Stock --> C`: If the `InventoryService` finds insufficient stock, the `UserMessageService` is notified to inform the user.
- `B -- Sufficient Stock --> D`: If the `InventoryService` finds sufficient stock, the `PaymentService` is invoked to authorize payment.
- `D -- Payment Authorized --> E`: If the payment is authorized, the `InventoryService` reserves the stock.
- `D -- Payment Declined --> F`: If the payment is declined, the `UserMessageService` is notified to inform the user.
- `E --> G`: After stock reservation, the `OrderService` creates a pending order.
- `G --> H`: The `OrderService` triggers the `DeliveryService` to schedule delivery.
- `H -- Delivery Scheduled --> I`: If the delivery is successfully scheduled, the `OrderService` confirms the order.
- `H -- Delivery Failed --> J`: If the delivery scheduling fails, the `CompensationService` begins the compensation flow.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **UserProfileService**: A service responsible for managing user profiles and initiating the purchase process.
- **InventoryService**: A service responsible for managing inventory and verifying stock availability.
- **PaymentService**: A service responsible for processing payments and authorizing transactions.
- **OrderService**: A service responsible for creating and managing orders.
- **DeliveryService**: A service responsible for scheduling and managing deliveries.
- **CompensationService**: A service responsible for handling compensation in case of failures.
- **Compensation Flow**: A process initiated when a transaction fails, involving refunds, credits, or other forms of compensation to the user.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The purchase process begins with the `UserProfileService` initiating a purchase, which immediately triggers the `InventoryService` to verify stock levels. A key decision point arises here: if there is insufficient stock, the flow branches to the `UserMessageService` to notify the user, effectively ending the purchase attempt. Conversely, if sufficient stock is available, the `PaymentService` is invoked to authorize the payment. Another branch occurs based on payment authorization: a declined payment results in the `UserMessageService` notifying the user, while an authorized payment leads to the `InventoryService` reserving the stock. Following successful stock reservation, the `OrderService` creates a pending order, which then prompts the `DeliveryService` to schedule the delivery. The final decision point is whether the delivery is successfully scheduled. If so, the `OrderService` confirms the order, completing the purchase flow. However, if delivery scheduling fails, the `CompensationService` initiates a compensation flow, potentially involving refunds or other remedies. This compensation flow is crucial for maintaining customer satisfaction in the event of logistical failures within the e-commerce system.
