## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The diagram illustrates a multi-threaded system involving a detector, camera, and serial communication. The detector thread begins at "detector start", then waits for a picture and serial data. The camera thread starts at "camera start", waiting for images from the camera and sending the data to the picture queue. The serial thread starts at "serial start", waiting for serial data and sending it to the serial data queue. The detector thread processes image and serial data to estimate posture and state, sending the results to the STM32 microcontroller. The STM32 sends data back to the detector thread. Each thread contains a loop, indicated by the "end a loop" nodes, which return to the initial waiting state. The "send data to stm32" node triggers a loop back to "wait for a picture" in the detector thread. The camera and serial threads similarly loop back to their respective waiting states after sending data to their queues. The STM32 microcontroller sends data to the "wait for a serial data" node in the detector thread, facilitating continuous processing. The queues act as buffers between the camera/serial threads and the detector thread, enabling asynchronous data processing.
