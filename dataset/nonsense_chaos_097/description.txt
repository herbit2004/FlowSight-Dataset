If the PreonFlux checkpoint fails, a VaultNode handshake is attempted; success leads to ArcLane Distributor Adjustment, also feeding into the ZenithGate challenge. A failed handshake (retry limit reached) terminates the process. The ZenithGate challenge branches based on authentication success; failure leads to termination, while success processes the NovaPulse Emission and allocates the CryoCore Segment. CoreSync Validation follows, and failure triggers termination. Successful validation initiates PrimeVector Calibration, leading to Data Integrity verification. If data integrity is verified, the process submits to the TemporalAxis Orchestrator, performs DeltaStream Averaging, and finalizes the ChrononLock Adjustment. If data integrity fails, the process terminates. The process ultimately ends at "End: LuminaChain Process Complete," which is reached either directly after ChrononLock Adjustment or after any of the termination conditions are met.
