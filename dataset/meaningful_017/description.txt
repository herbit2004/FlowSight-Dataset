## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The payment gateway callback triggers the signature and timestamp validation process.
- `B -- Valid --> C`: If the signature and timestamp are valid, the callback data is deserialized.
- `B -- Invalid --> E`: If the signature and timestamp are invalid, a security alert is logged.
- `C --> D`: The deserialized callback data is used to determine the payment status.
- `D -- Yes --> F`: If the payment status is "Success", the payment record is updated to "Settled".
- `D -- No --> G`: If the payment status is not "Success", the payment status is checked for "Pending".
- `G -- Yes --> H`: If the payment status is "Pending", the payment record is updated to "Pending".
- `G -- No --> I`: If the payment status is not "Pending", the payment record is updated to "Failed".
- `F --> J`: After updating the payment record to "Settled", a reconciliation job is triggered.
- `I --> J`: After updating the payment record to "Failed", a reconciliation job is triggered.
- `H --> J`: After updating the payment record to "Pending", a reconciliation job is triggered.
Multiple paths converge at node J.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Payment Gateway Callback**: A notification sent by a payment gateway to a merchant's system to inform about the status of a payment transaction.
- **Signature Validation**: The process of verifying the authenticity of a message or data by checking its digital signature.
- **Timestamp Validation**: The process of verifying that a message or data was created within a reasonable timeframe to prevent replay attacks.
- **Deserialize**: The process of converting data from a serialized format (e.g., JSON, XML) back into its original data structure.
- **Payment Status**: The current state of a payment transaction (e.g., Success, Pending, Failed).
- **Reconciliation Job**: A process that compares and matches payment records between different systems (e.g., merchant's system and payment gateway) to ensure consistency and accuracy.
- **Security Alert**: A notification indicating a potential security issue or threat.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with the "Payment Gateway Callback," triggering the "Validate Signature & Timestamp" node. If the signature and timestamp are deemed "Invalid," a "Log Security Alert" is triggered, halting further processing along that branch. If "Valid," the flow proceeds to "Deserialize Callback Data," preparing the data for evaluation. The next key decision point is "Payment Status: Success?". A "Yes" branch leads to "Update Payment Record: 'Settled'," while a "No" branch proceeds to "Payment Status: Pending?". If "Yes" at the pending status check, the "Update Payment Record: 'Pending'" node is activated; if "No," the "Update Payment Record: 'Failed'" node is triggered. Regardless of whether the payment record is updated to 'Settled', 'Failed', or 'Pending', the process converges at the "Trigger Reconciliation Job" node. The reconciliation job is crucial for ensuring data consistency between the merchant's system and the payment gateway, a standard practice in payment processing to mitigate discrepancies and potential financial losses. The signature validation step protects against malicious actors replaying old payment requests.
