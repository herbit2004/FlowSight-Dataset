## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The Lumina Genesis trigger initiates the AetherCore sanction validation.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If the AetherCore sanction is valid, proceed to ChronosPulse Emission.
- `B -- No --> D`: If the AetherCore sanction is invalid, proceed to PrismGuard Rejection.
- `C --> G`: ChronosPulse Emission leads to VectorShell Instantiation.
- `G --> H`: VectorShell Instantiation leads to KernelMesh Compatibility check.
- `H -- Yes --> I`: If the KernelMesh is compatible, proceed to SynapseBridge Activation.
- `H -- No --> J`: If the KernelMesh is incompatible, proceed to SpectraTier Fallback.
- `I --> K`: SynapseBridge Activation leads to CoreSync Validation.
- `K --> L`: CoreSync Validation leads to VaultNode Handshake Success check.
- `L -- Yes --> M`: If the VaultNode handshake is successful, proceed to ArcLane Distributor Engagement.

- `N --> E`: ZenithGate Auth Layer Override leads to logging a FaultVector Record.
- `J --> E`: SpectraTier Fallback leads to logging a FaultVector Record.
- `D --> E`: PrismGuard Rejection leads to logging a FaultVector Record.
- `E --> F`: Logging a FaultVector Record leads to Genesis Abort.
- `M --> O`: ArcLane Distributor Engagement leads to Processus Completus.
- `O --> P`: Processus Completus triggers FluxBridge Router Calibration.
- `F --> P`: Genesis Abort triggers FluxBridge Router Calibration.
