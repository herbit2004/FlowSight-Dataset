## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram. It depicts the order processing workflow, from when a user submits an order to when the order is closed and inventory is updated. The diagram outlines the steps involved in fulfilling an order, handling inventory shortages, and resolving delivery issues.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) direction. The start node is "User Submits Order," and the end nodes are "Update Inventory". The diagram shows a linear flow with decision points that can lead to different paths, including loops and cancellation scenarios.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: User submits an order, initiating the workflow.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: Payment is received, proceeding to order acknowledgement.
- `B -- No --> F`: Payment is not received, leading to order cancellation.
- `C --> D`: Order is acknowledged, proceeding to inventory availability check.
- `D -- Yes --> E`: Inventory is available, leading to order shipment.
- `D -- No --> G`: Inventory is not available, leading to shortage notification.
- `G --> H`: Shortage is notified, proceeding to attempt restock.
- `H --> I`: Attempt to restock inventory.
- `I -- Yes --> D`: Restock is successful, incorrectly looping back to "Inventory Available?".

- `E --> J`: Order is shipped, proceeding to send shipping confirmation.
- `J --> K`: Shipping confirmation is sent, proceeding to delivery success check.

- `L --> M`: Delivery is investigated, proceeding to resolution check.
- `M -- Yes --> J`: Resolution is found, proceeding to send shipping confirmation again.
- `M -- No --> F`: Resolution is not found, leading to order cancellation.
- `F --> N`: Order is cancelled, proceeding to refund payment.
- `N --> O`: Payment is refunded, proceeding to close order.
- `O --> P`: Order is closed, proceeding to update inventory.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Order**: A request from a customer to purchase goods or services. In this diagram, it represents the entire process from submission to closure.
- **Payment**: The act of transferring money in exchange for goods or services. Here, it's a binary check: received or not.
- **Inventory**: The stock of goods available for sale. In this diagram, it's checked for availability and updated after the order is closed.
- **Restock**: The process of replenishing inventory. In this diagram, it's attempted when a shortage is detected.
- **Shipping Confirmation**: A notification sent to the customer confirming that their order has been shipped. In this diagram, it's sent after the order is shipped.
- **Delivery**: The process of transporting goods to the customer. In this diagram, its success is checked, and failures are investigated.
- **Refund**: The act of returning money to the customer. In this diagram, it's issued when an order is cancelled.
