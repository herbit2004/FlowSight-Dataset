## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating a research and report generation process. It outlines the steps from initial research query to the final report with sources, emphasizing iterative research and synthesis. The diagram serves as a high-level overview of the workflow.

## Overall Layout
The diagram flows from left to right. It is divided into four stages: Input, Iterative Research, Synthesis, and Output. The process starts with the "Research Query" node and ends with the "Final Report with Sources" node.

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram contains four explicit subgraphs:
- **Input**: Represents the initial stage where the research query is provided.
- **Iterative Research**: Encompasses the steps of generating questions, parallel searching, and analyzing results, with a feedback loop for needing more information.
- **Synthesis**: Includes the generation of the report and the addition of citations.
- **Output**: Represents the final stage where the final report with sources is produced.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `Q --> GEN`: The research query leads to the generation of questions.
- `GEN --> SEARCH`: Generated questions lead to parallel search.
- `SEARCH --> ANALYZE`: Parallel search results are analyzed.
- `ANALYZE --> REPORT`: Analyzed results lead to the generation of the report.
- `ANALYZE -.-> GEN`: If more information is needed, the process loops back to generating questions.
- `REPORT --> CITE`: The generated report leads to the addition of citations.
- `CITE --> RESULT`: The report with citations leads to the final report with sources.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Research Query**: The initial question or topic that drives the research process.
- **Parallel Search**: Conducting multiple searches simultaneously to gather information efficiently.
- **Citations**: References to sources used in the report, providing credibility and allowing readers to verify information.
- **Final Report with Sources**: The complete research document including all relevant information and properly cited sources.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with a "Research Query" as input, which initiates the "Iterative Research" phase. The first step in this phase is to "Generate Questions" based on the query. These questions then drive a "Parallel Search" for relevant information. The "Analyze Results" node represents the analysis of the search findings. A key branch exists here: if more information is needed, indicated by the "Need more info?" condition, the process loops back to "Generate Questions," creating an iterative research cycle. Once sufficient information is gathered, the process moves to the "Synthesis" phase, starting with "Generate Report" followed by "Add Citations." Finally, the "Output" phase produces the "Final Report with Sources." This final report is the culmination of the research, synthesis, and citation process, providing a comprehensive document with properly attributed information. The iterative nature of the research phase allows for refinement and deeper exploration of the topic, ensuring a thorough and well-supported final report.
