## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the expense report approval process. It outlines the steps involved in submitting, reviewing, and approving expense reports, including checks for manager approval, finance review, and compliance. The diagram serves as a visual guide to understand the workflow and decision points in the expense management system.

## Overall Layout
The diagram flows from top to bottom. It starts with submitting an expense report and ends with either the report being approved or the submitter being notified of rejection or non-compliance. The diagram uses diamond shapes to represent decision points and rectangular shapes for processes or actions.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Submitting an expense report triggers the manager approval process.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If manager approval is required, the process proceeds to finance review.
- `B -- No --> D`: If manager approval is not required, the report is rejected.
- `C -- Yes --> E`: If finance review is required, the process proceeds to compliance check.
- `C -- No --> F`: If finance review is not required, more information is requested.
- `F --> C`: After requesting more information, the process returns to finance review.
- `E -- Pass --> G`: If the compliance check passes, the process proceeds to payment processing.
- `E -- Fail --> H`: If the compliance check fails, the report is rejected due to non-compliance.
- `G --> I`: Payment processing leads to generating a payment file.
- `I --> J`: Generating a payment file leads to uploading it to the bank system.
- `J --> K`: Uploading to the bank system leads to payment confirmation.
- `H --> L`: Compliance rejection leads to notifying the submitter of non-compliance.
- `L --> A`: After notifying the submitter of non-compliance, the process returns to submitting an expense report.
- `D --> M`: Report rejection leads to notifying the submitter of rejection.
- `M --> A`: After notifying the submitter of rejection, the process returns to submitting an expense report.
- `K --> N`: Payment confirmation leads to updating the expense system.
- `N --> O`: Updating the expense system leads to the report being approved.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Expense Report**: A document detailing expenses incurred by an employee or individual, submitted for reimbursement.
- **Manager Approval**: The process where a manager reviews and approves an expense report.
- **Finance Review**: The process where the finance department reviews an expense report for accuracy and adherence to company policies.
- **Compliance Check**: The process of verifying that the expense report complies with relevant regulations and internal policies.
- **Payment Processing**: The process of initiating and completing the payment for the approved expense report.
- **Payment File**: A file containing payment instructions for the bank.
- **Bank System**: The system used by the bank to process payments.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The expense report approval process begins with the submitter creating and submitting an expense report (A). The first decision point is whether manager approval is required (B). If yes, the report proceeds to finance review (C); if not, the report is rejected (D), and the submitter is notified (M), restarting the process. If finance review is required and passed, a compliance check (E) is performed. A failed compliance check results in compliance rejection (H), notification to the submitter (L), and a restart of the process. A passed compliance check leads to payment processing (G), generating a payment file (I), and uploading it to the bank system (J). Once payment is confirmed (K), the expense system is updated (N), and the report is marked as approved (O). If finance review is not required, more information is requested (F), and the process loops back to finance review (C). The process ends with either the report being approved (O) or the submitter being notified of rejection (M) or non-compliance (L), each of which loops back to the initial submission step (A). The compliance check (E) ensures that all expenses adhere to company policies and regulatory requirements, a critical step in maintaining financial integrity.
