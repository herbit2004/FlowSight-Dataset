## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The Cron Scheduler triggers the Job Ready check.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If the job is ready, it is dispatched to the Job Dispatcher.
- `B -- No --> A`: If the job is not ready, the process loops back to the Cron Scheduler.
- `C --> D`: The Job Dispatcher initiates Pre-Dispatch Checks.
- `D -- Passed --> E`: If Pre-Dispatch Checks pass, resources are allocated.
- `D -- Failed --> F`: If Pre-Dispatch Checks fail, the error is logged.
- `E --> G`: Resource Allocator passes control to the Job Executor.
- `G --> H`: The Job Executor completes and reports Post-Execution Status.
- `H -- Success --> I`: If the job execution is successful, the results are processed.
- `H -- Failure --> J`: If the job execution fails, the Retry Manager is invoked.
- `I --> K`: The Result Processor triggers the Event Publisher.
- `J --> B`: The Retry Manager loops back to the Job Ready? check.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Cron Scheduler**: A time-based job scheduler in computer operating systems. It enables users to schedule jobs (commands or scripts) to run automatically at specific times, dates, or intervals.
- **Job Dispatcher**: A component responsible for receiving job requests and distributing them to available worker nodes or execution environments.
- **Pre-Dispatch Checks**: Validation steps performed before a job is executed, ensuring that all necessary conditions and dependencies are met.
- **Resource Allocator**: A system that manages and allocates computing resources (e.g., CPU, memory, storage) to jobs or tasks.
- **Job Executor**: The component that runs the actual job or task, performing the required computations or operations.
- **Post-Execution Status**: The status of a job after it has been executed, indicating whether it was successful, failed, or encountered errors.
- **Result Processor**: A module that processes the output or results generated by a job, often involving data transformation, aggregation, or analysis.
- **Event Publisher**: A component that publishes events or notifications related to job execution, allowing other systems or services to react to job status changes.
- **Retry Manager**: A system that automatically retries failed job executions, typically with configurable retry policies and backoff strategies.
