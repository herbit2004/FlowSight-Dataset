## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `Dock Arrival Notification --> Unload Truck?`: Upon arrival, determine if the truck should be unloaded.
- `Unload Truck? -- Yes --> Initiate Pallet Scan`: If yes, initiate pallet scanning.
- `Unload Truck? -- No --> Hold for Review`: If no, hold for review.
- `Initiate Pallet Scan --> Verify ASN Data`: After scanning, verify the ASN data.
- `Verify ASN Data -- Match --> Move to Staging Area`: If the ASN data matches, move to the staging area.
- `Verify ASN Data -- Mismatch --> Exception Handling`: If the ASN data does not match, handle the exception.
- `Move to Staging Area --> QA Sampling Required?`: After moving to the staging area, determine if QA sampling is required.
- `QA Sampling Required? -- Yes --> Perform QA Inspection`: If yes, perform QA inspection.
- `QA Sampling Required? -- No --> Release to Putaway`: If no, release to putaway.
- `Perform QA Inspection -- Pass --> Release to Putaway`: If the inspection passes, release to putaway.
- `Perform QA Inspection -- Fail --> Quarantine & Investigate`: If the inspection fails, quarantine and investigate.
- `Release to Putaway --> Assign Putaway Task`: After release, assign a putaway task.
- `Assign Putaway Task --> Move Pallet to Location`: Move the pallet to its location.
- `Move Pallet to Location --> Confirm Putaway Completion`: Confirm putaway completion.
- `Confirm Putaway Completion --> Update Inventory System`: Update the inventory system.
- `Order Received --> Allocate Inventory`: Upon receiving an order, allocate inventory.
- `Allocate Inventory -- Sufficient --> Generate Picking List`: If sufficient inventory is available, generate a picking list.
- `Allocate Inventory -- Insufficient --> Backorder Management`: If insufficient inventory is available, manage backorders.
- `Generate Picking List --> Pick from Location`: After generating the picking list, pick from the location.
- `Pick from Location --> Verification Scan`: After picking, perform a verification scan.
- `Verification Scan --> Consolidate & Pack`: After verification, consolidate and pack.
- `Consolidate & Pack --> Generate Shipping Label`: After consolidating and packing, generate a shipping label.
- `Generate Shipping Label --> Load onto Outbound Carrier`: After generating the label, load onto the outbound carrier.
- `Load onto Outbound Carrier --> Shipment Confirmation`: After loading, confirm the shipment.
- `Exception Handling --> Investigate Discrepancy`: After exception handling, investigate the discrepancy.
- `Quarantine & Investigate --> Investigate Discrepancy`: After quarantine and investigation, investigate the discrepancy.
- `Backorder Management --> Notify Procurement`: After backorder management, notify procurement.
- `Notify Procurement --> Allocate Inventory`: After notifying procurement, allocate inventory.
- `Update Inventory System --> Inventory Monitoring`: After updating the inventory system, monitor inventory.
- `Shipment Confirmation --> Inventory Monitoring`: After shipment confirmation, monitor inventory.
- `Investigate Discrepancy --> Verify ASN Data`: After investigating the discrepancy, verify the ASN data.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **ASN (Advanced Shipping Notice)**: A document that provides detailed information about an impending delivery.
- **Putaway**: The process of moving received goods to their designated storage locations in a warehouse.
- **Backorder**: An order that cannot be fulfilled immediately due to insufficient inventory.
- **Picking List**: A document that lists the items to be picked from the warehouse to fulfill an order.
- **SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**: A unique identifier for a particular item in inventory.
- **Inventory System**: A system used to track the quantity and location of items in inventory.
- **Procurement**: The process of acquiring goods or services.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The supply chain process starts with either a "Dock Arrival Notification" at the receiving dock or an "Order Received" for outbound processing. For receiving, the process branches immediately based on whether the truck should be unloaded; if yes, "Initiate Pallet Scan," otherwise "Hold for Review." Scanned pallets proceed to "Verify ASN Data," branching based on a match or mismatch. A mismatch triggers "Exception Handling" and subsequent "Investigate Discrepancy," looping back to "Verify ASN Data." A match leads to "Move to Staging Area" and a "QA Sampling Required?" decision. If QA is required, "Perform QA Inspection," branching on pass/fail to "Release to Putaway" or "Quarantine & Investigate," with the latter also leading to "Investigate Discrepancy." The "Release to Putaway" path enters the "Putaway Process," sequentially executing "Assign Putaway Task," "Move Pallet to Location," "Confirm Putaway Completion," and "Update Inventory System." For outbound processing, "Order Received" leads to "Allocate Inventory," branching on "Sufficient" or "Insufficient" inventory. Insufficient inventory triggers "Backorder Management" and "Notify Procurement," looping back to "Allocate Inventory." Sufficient inventory generates a "Generate Picking List," followed by "Pick from Location," "Verification Scan," and "Consolidate & Pack." This leads to the "Shipping Dock" processes: "Generate Shipping Label," "Load onto Outbound Carrier," and "Shipment Confirmation." Both "Update Inventory System" and "Shipment Confirmation" feed into "Inventory Monitoring," the process's end. The ASN data verification is a key control point, ensuring that received goods match the expected shipment details.
