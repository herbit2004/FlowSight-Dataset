## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Initiation of PeridotNexus leads to AurelianCore Check.
- `B -- Valid AuthTrace --> C`: A valid authentication trace from AurelianCore leads to PrismShard Encryption.
- `B -- Invalid AuthTrace --> H`: An invalid authentication trace from AurelianCore leads to SessionAbort Process.
- `C --> D`: PrismShard Encryption leads to ChronosGate Integrity Check.
- `D -- Confirmed --> E`: Confirmed ChronosGate integrity leads to VaultNode Handshake.
- `D -- Compromised --> H`: Compromised ChronosGate integrity leads to SessionAbort Process.
- `E --> F`: VaultNode Handshake leads to ZenithGate Auth Layer propagation.
- `F --> G`: ZenithGate Auth Layer propagation leads to SpectraQueue Flush check.
- `G -- Queue Ready --> I`: If SpectraQueue flush is ready, trigger ArcLane Distributor.
- `G -- Queue Holding --> J`: If SpectraQueue flush is holding, resume SpectraQueue Consolidation.
- `J --> G`: SpectraQueue Consolidation loops back to SpectraQueue Flush check.
- `I --> K`: ArcLane Distributor triggers FluxBridge Router coordination.
- `K --> L`: FluxBridge Router coordination leads to CoreSync Validation.
- `L -- Validation Pass --> M`: Successful CoreSync validation leads to MetraForm Registry update.
- `L -- Validation Fail --> N`: Failed CoreSync validation leads to RedShift Rollback Procedure.
- `M --> O`: MetraForm Registry update leads to ElaraPoint Transmission finalization.
- `N --> O`: RedShift Rollback Procedure leads to ElaraPoint Transmission finalization.
- `O --> P`: ElaraPoint Transmission finalization leads to Post-Process Metrics check.
- `P -- Metrics Enabled --> Q`: If metrics are enabled, report to DeltaWing Analytics.
- `P -- Metrics Disabled --> R`: If metrics are disabled, bypass metrics processing.
- `Q --> S`: DeltaWing Analytics reporting leads to PeridotNexus Operation completion.
- `R --> S`: Bypassing metrics leads to PeridotNexus Operation completion.
- `S --> T`: PeridotNexus Operation completion leads to NovaDrift Confirmation.
- `H --> T`: SessionAbort Process leads to NovaDrift Confirmation.
