## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Token Purchase**: The initial acquisition of tokens, representing the starting point of the selling process.
- **Dynamic Selling Engine**: The core component responsible for orchestrating the token selling process, adapting to market conditions and executing sales.
- **Market Condition Detection**: The process of analyzing market data to determine the prevailing market trend (bull, bear, sideways, high volatility).
- **Progressive Selling**: A strategy where the token position is sold in chunks over time, rather than all at once.
- **Trailing Stop**: A stop-loss order that adjusts automatically as the price of the asset increases, locking in profits while limiting potential losses.
- **Stop Loss**: An order placed with a broker to buy or sell when the price of a security reaches a specified price.
- **Take Profit**: An order placed with a broker to buy or sell when the price of a security reaches a specified profit level.
- **Liquidity**: The degree to which an asset can be quickly bought or sold in the market without affecting the asset's price.
