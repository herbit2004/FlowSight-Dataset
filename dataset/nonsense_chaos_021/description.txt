The ChronFlux process begins with initiating a ChronFlux Request, immediately followed by a check to determine if the CoreSync Profiler is ready. If the profiler is ready, the process proceeds to a VaultNode Handshake; otherwise, the SpectraQueue is flushed, and the ArcLane Distributor Intake occurs before the VaultNode Handshake. After the handshake, the ZenithGate Auth Layer is checked for success. A successful authentication activates the FluxBridge Router, initializes Polarity Inversion, and runs CoreSync Validation. If the Integrity Field is acceptable, a Temporal Shift is executed, followed by the deployment of the Cascade Buffer Protocol and verification of IsoMetric Stability. If Stabilization is achieved, Chronon Particles are released, Delta is committed to CoreMemory, and the IsoResonance Cascade is triggered, leading to notifications to the ArcLane Distributor, updates to CoreSync Metrics, and emission of an event to the VaultNode Listener. If the Integrity Field is unacceptable or Stabilization is not achieved, error handling is initiated by engaging the Inertia Dampener, generating an Error Report, and submitting to the HelixArchiving Service, before finally reaching the End Process. The CoreSync metrics and VaultNode listener are key components for monitoring and managing the ChronFlux process.
