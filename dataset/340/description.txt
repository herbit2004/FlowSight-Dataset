## Repository Context & Terminology
- **LLM (Large Language Model)**: A deep learning model trained on a massive dataset of text and code, capable of generating human-quality text, translating languages, and answering questions.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: A framework that combines the strengths of pre-trained language models with information retrieval techniques to improve the accuracy and relevance of generated text.
- **Vector Search**: A search technique that uses vector embeddings to find similar items in a database.
- **SurrealDB**: A cloud, distributed, collaborative, graph database, suitable for RAG/Vector Search.
- **NCBI (National Center for Biotechnology Information)**: A part of the United States National Library of Medicine (NLM), a branch of the National Institutes of Health (NIH). It hosts databases such as PubMed.
- **clinicaltrials.gov**: A registry and results database of publicly and privately supported clinical studies of human participants conducted around the world.
- **medlineplus.gov**: An online health information resource for patients and their families and friends. It is a service of the National Library of Medicine (NLM), the world's largest medical library, which is part of the National Institutes of Health (NIH).
