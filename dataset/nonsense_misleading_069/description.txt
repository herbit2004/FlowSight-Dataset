Upon successful payment ("Success"), the system "Generate Shipping Label," then "Pack Order," and then "Order Shipped." After the "Order Shipped" step, the system proceeds to "Deliver Order," which then creates an illogical loop back to "Inventory Check." The standard e-commerce concept of order placement initiates the workflow, but the subsequent steps deviate from typical practices, particularly with the circular flow after delivery and the shipping of out-of-stock items. The diagram lacks a proper termination point, with processes looping back to earlier stages, creating a non-terminating workflow.
