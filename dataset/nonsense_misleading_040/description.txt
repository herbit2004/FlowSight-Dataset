## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary

If an order is received, the process moves to "Check Inventory". If the inventory is unavailable, the customer is notified of the shortage, and the order is closed. If the order is not delivered, the process enters an "Investigate Delay" loop, returning to "Ship Order".
