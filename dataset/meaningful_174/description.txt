## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the architecture and flow of a question answering system. It depicts the process of handling a user query, classifying its intent, retrieving relevant information from various knowledge sources, and generating a response. The diagram serves as a high-level overview of the system's components and their interactions.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's primary direction is top-to-bottom (TD). It is hierarchically structured into three main stages: User Interaction, Knowledge Graph & Databases, and Retrieval & Ranking. The start node is "User Query," and the end node is "User Response."

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram contains three explicit subgraphs:
- **User Interaction**: Handles the initial user query, preprocessing, intent classification, and clarification prompts.
- **Knowledge Graph & Databases**: Manages the knowledge sources (FAQ database, procedure knowledge graph, concept ontology) and performs reasoning using graph traversal and ontology inference.
- **Retrieval & Ranking**: Ranks the retrieved information from different sources and generates a final response.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `User Query --> Query Preprocessing`: The user query is passed to the query preprocessing stage.
- `Query Preprocessing --> Intent Classification`: The preprocessed query is passed to the intent classification stage.
- `Intent Classification -- Intent: FAQ --> FAQ Retrieval`: If the intent is classified as FAQ, the flow proceeds to FAQ retrieval.
- `Intent Classification -- Intent: Procedural --> Procedure Retrieval`: If the intent is classified as procedural, the flow proceeds to procedure retrieval.
- `Intent Classification -- Intent: Conceptual --> Concept Retrieval`: If the intent is classified as conceptual, the flow proceeds to concept retrieval.
- `Intent Classification -- Intent: Unclear/Ambiguous --> Clarification Prompt`: If the intent is unclear or ambiguous, the flow proceeds to a clarification prompt.
- `Clarification Prompt --> Query Preprocessing`: The clarified query is passed back to the query preprocessing stage.
- `FAQ Retrieval --> FAQ Database`: The FAQ retrieval stage accesses the FAQ database.
- `Procedure Retrieval --> Procedure Knowledge Graph`: The procedure retrieval stage accesses the procedure knowledge graph.
- `Concept Retrieval --> Concept Ontology`: The concept retrieval stage accesses the concept ontology.
- `Procedure Knowledge Graph --> Rule Engine`: The procedure knowledge graph is used by the rule engine.
- `Rule Engine -- Rule Matched --> Procedure Step Recommendation`: If a rule is matched, the flow proceeds to procedure step recommendation.
- `Rule Engine -- No Rule Matched --> Fallback Procedure Suggestion`: If no rule is matched, the flow proceeds to fallback procedure suggestion.
- `Procedure Step Recommendation --> Postprocessing & Formatting`: The procedure step recommendation is postprocessed and formatted.
- `Fallback Procedure Suggestion --> Postprocessing & Formatting`: The fallback procedure suggestion is postprocessed and formatted.
- `Concept Ontology --> Inference Engine`: The concept ontology is used by the inference engine.
- `Inference Engine -- Inference Successful --> Relevant Concept Expansion`: If inference is successful, the flow proceeds to relevant concept expansion.
- `Inference Engine -- Inference Failed --> Related Concept Retrieval`: If inference fails, the flow proceeds to related concept retrieval.
- `Relevant Concept Expansion --> Concept Summarization`: The relevant concepts are summarized.
- `Related Concept Retrieval --> Concept Summarization`: The related concepts are summarized.
- `Concept Summarization --> Postprocessing & Formatting`: The summarized concepts are postprocessed and formatted.
- `FAQ Database --> Similarity Scoring`: The FAQ database is used for similarity scoring.
- `Procedure Step Recommendation --> Contextualization`: The procedure step recommendation is contextualized.
- `Fallback Procedure Suggestion --> Contextualization`: The fallback procedure suggestion is contextualized.
- `Concept Summarization --> Relevance Filtering`: The summarized concepts are used for relevance filtering.
- `Similarity Scoring --> FAQ Ranking`: The similarity scores are used for FAQ ranking.
- `Contextualization --> Procedure Ranking`: The contextualization is used for procedure ranking.
- `Relevance Filtering --> Concept Ranking`: The relevance filtering is used for concept ranking.
- `FAQ Ranking --> Fusion & Ranking`: The ranked FAQs are fused and ranked.
- `Procedure Ranking --> Fusion & Ranking`: The ranked procedures are fused and ranked.
- `Concept Ranking --> Fusion & Ranking`: The ranked concepts are fused and ranked.
- `Postprocessing & Formatting (from Graph Traversal & Reasoning) --> Contextualization`: Output from graph traversal and reasoning is fed into contextualization.
- `Postprocessing & Formatting (from Ontology Reasoning) --> Relevance Filtering`: Output from ontology reasoning is fed into relevance filtering.
- `Fusion & Ranking --> Response Generation`: The fused and ranked information is used for response generation.
- `Response Generation --> User Response`: The generated response is presented to the user.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **User Query**: The question or request submitted by the user to the system.
- **Intent Classification**: The process of determining the user's goal or purpose behind the query.
- **Knowledge Graph**: A structured representation of knowledge, consisting of entities, concepts, and their relationships.
- **Ontology**: A formal representation of knowledge as a set of concepts within a domain and the relationships between those concepts.
- **Inference Engine**: A component that applies logical rules to infer new knowledge from existing knowledge.
- **Retrieval & Ranking**: The process of identifying and ordering relevant information from various sources based on their relevance to the user query.
- **Response Generation**: The process of creating a coherent and informative answer to the user's query based on the retrieved and ranked information.
- **FAQ**: Frequently Asked Question.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The question answering system begins with a "User Query," which is then passed to "Query Preprocessing" to prepare it for "Intent Classification." The "Intent Classification" stage branches based on the identified intent: "Intent: FAQ" leads to "FAQ Retrieval," "Intent: Procedural" leads to "Procedure Retrieval," "Intent: Conceptual" leads to "Concept Retrieval," and "Intent: Unclear/Ambiguous" leads to a "Clarification Prompt," which loops back to "Query Preprocessing." The "FAQ Retrieval" accesses the "FAQ Database." The "Procedure Retrieval" accesses the "Procedure Knowledge Graph," which feeds into a "Rule Engine." The "Rule Engine" branches based on whether a rule is matched, leading to either "Procedure Step Recommendation" or "Fallback Procedure Suggestion," both of which are then processed by "Postprocessing & Formatting." The "Concept Retrieval" accesses the "Concept Ontology," which is used by an "Inference Engine." The "Inference Engine" branches based on the success of inference, leading to either "Relevant Concept Expansion" or "Related Concept Retrieval," both of which are then summarized by "Concept Summarization" and processed by "Postprocessing & Formatting." The outputs from the graph traversal and ontology reasoning stages are then contextualized and filtered, respectively. The system then performs "Similarity Scoring" for FAQs, "Contextualization" for procedures, and "Relevance Filtering" for concepts, followed by ranking each type of information. The ranked information is then fused and ranked in "Fusion & Ranking," which feeds into "Response Generation," ultimately producing the "User Response." The system leverages a knowledge graph and ontology to reason about user queries and provide relevant answers.
