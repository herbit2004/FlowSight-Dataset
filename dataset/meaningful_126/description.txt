## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Customer support ticket is sent to the ticket classification engine.
- `B -- High Priority --> C`: High-priority tickets are routed to the Escalation Manager.
- `B -- Low Priority --> D`: Low-priority tickets are routed to the Knowledge Base Lookup.
- `C --> E`: The Escalation Manager creates an escalation task.
- `E --> F`: The escalation is assigned to Tier 2 support.
- `F --> G`: Tier 2 support is notified.
- `G --> H`: Checks Tier 2 availability.
- `H -- Available --> I`: If Tier 2 is available, the ticket is assigned.
- `H -- Unavailable --> J`: If Tier 2 is unavailable, the ticket is queued for Tier 2.
- `D -- Article Found --> K`: If an article is found in the knowledge base, it is sent to the customer.
- `D -- No Article Found --> L`: If no article is found, the ticket is routed to Tier 1 support.
- `K --> M`: The ticket is closed as resolved.
- `L --> N`: Checks if the ticket handling time exceeds the threshold.
- `N -- Yes --> O`: If the handling time exceeds the threshold, the ticket is flagged for supervisor review.
- `N -- No --> P`: If the handling time does not exceed the threshold, Tier 1 support continues.
- `I --> U`: The ticket is assigned to an agent.
- `U --> P`: The ticket is handled by Tier 1 support.
- `P --> Q`: Checks if the customer is satisfied.
- `Q -- Yes --> M`: If the customer is satisfied, the ticket is closed.
- `Q -- No --> R`: If the customer is not satisfied, the ticket is re-opened and escalation details are added.
- `R --> S`: Details are added to the escalation context.
- `S --> F`: The escalation is assigned to Tier 2 support.
- `O --> V`: The ticket is reviewed by a supervisor.
- `V --> W`: Checks if the ticket should be escalated to Tier 2.
- `W -- Yes --> S`: If the ticket should be escalated, details are added to the escalation context.
- `W -- No --> P`: If the ticket should not be escalated, Tier 1 support continues.
- `J --> H`: The ticket is requeued for Tier 2 availability.
- `I --> P`: The ticket is handled by Tier 1 support.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Customer Support Ticket**: A record of a customer's issue or request for assistance.
- **Ticket Classification Engine**: A system that automatically categorizes support tickets based on their content.
- **Escalation Manager**: A component responsible for managing the escalation of high-priority or complex tickets to higher support tiers.
- **Knowledge Base**: A repository of articles and documentation that can help resolve common customer issues.
- **Tier 1 Support**: The first level of support, typically handling basic issues and providing initial assistance.
- **Tier 2 Support**: A higher level of support, typically handling more complex issues that require specialized knowledge.
- **Ticket Handling Time**: The duration of time spent resolving a customer support ticket.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The flowchart begins with a "Customer Support Ticket Received," which is then processed by the "Ticket Classification Engine." Based on the classification, the ticket is either routed as "High Priority" to the "Escalation Manager," leading to the creation of an "Escalation Task" and assignment to "Tier 2 Support," or as "Low Priority" to "Knowledge Base Lookup." If an "Article Found" in the knowledge base, it's sent to the customer, and the ticket is closed. If "No Article Found," the ticket is routed to "Tier 1 Support." A key decision point is "Ticket Handling Time > Threshold?," which, if "Yes," flags the ticket for "Supervisor Review," otherwise, "Tier 1 Support" continues. "Tier 2 Availability?" determines whether the ticket is immediately assigned or queued. After assignment to an agent, the process checks "Customer Satisfied?," closing the ticket if "Yes," or re-opening and escalating details if "No." The "Escalation Manager" component is responsible for creating escalation tasks for high-priority tickets, ensuring they receive prompt attention from specialized support tiers. The process ultimately aims to resolve the customer's issue and close the ticket, with escalation and supervisor review steps in place to handle complex or time-sensitive cases.
