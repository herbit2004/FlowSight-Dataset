## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating a support ticket resolution process. It depicts the steps involved from the initial submission of a support ticket by a user to the eventual closure of the ticket. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the workflow, outlining the various stages and decision points in the ticket resolution process.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's layout is top-down (TD). It starts with the "User submits support ticket" node at the top and flows downwards through a series of decision diamonds and process rectangles. The process ends with the "Close ticket" node, which loops back to the start.

No explicit subgraphs; logically divided into 6 stages: Ticket Submission & SLA Check, Initial Triage, Knowledge Base Resolution, Specialist Escalation, Resolution & Closure, and Knowledge Base Update.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: User submits a support ticket, initiating the process.
- `B -- No --> C`: If the SLA is not breached, the ticket proceeds to triage and categorization.
- `B -- Yes --> H`: If the SLA is breached, the issue is immediately resolved and the ticket is closed.
- `C --> D`: After triage, the process checks for knowledge base availability.
- `D -- No --> E`: If no knowledge base article is available, the ticket is escalated to a specialist team.
- `D -- Yes --> I`: If a knowledge base article is available, it is provided to the user.
- `I --> J`: The user is provided with a KB article.
- `J -- No --> E`: If the user does not confirm the resolution, the ticket is escalated to a specialist team.
- `J -- Yes --> H`: If the user confirms the resolution, the issue is resolved and the ticket is closed.
- `E --> F`: The specialist team attempts to resolve the issue.
- `F -- No --> G`: If the specialist resolution is unsuccessful, user receipt is acknowledged.
- `F -- Yes --> H`: If the specialist resolution is successful, the issue is resolved and the ticket is closed.
- `H --> K`: After resolving the issue and closing the ticket, the knowledge base is updated.
- `K --> L`: The knowledge base update leads to closing the ticket.
- `L --> A`: Closing the ticket restarts the process at ticket submission.
- `G --> L`: Acknowledging user receipt leads directly to closing the ticket.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: A commitment between a service provider and a client. In this diagram, an SLA breach immediately leads to issue resolution, bypassing triage, which is an error.
- **Triage**: The process of prioritizing and categorizing support tickets. In this diagram, triage occurs only if the SLA is not breached.
- **Knowledge Base (KB)**: A repository of information and solutions to common problems. In this diagram, the KB is consulted after triage.
- **Specialist Team**: A team of experts who handle escalated support tickets. In this diagram, escalation occurs if no KB article is available or if the user doesn't confirm resolution.
- **Ticket Resolution**: The process of resolving the user's issue. In this diagram, resolution can occur through KB articles or specialist intervention.
- **Ticket Closure**: The final step in the support process. In this diagram, closing the ticket restarts the entire process.
- **User Receipt Acknowledgement**: A confirmation to the user that their issue has been received. In this diagram, this leads directly to ticket closure without resolution.
