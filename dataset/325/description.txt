## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is an architecture diagram depicting the flow of data and decisions within a trading system. It illustrates the different layers and agents involved in collecting market data, analyzing it, making predictions, assessing risk, and executing trades. The diagram serves as a high-level overview of the system's components and their interactions.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) fashion, showing the progression of data and decisions from the Data Collection Layer at the top to the Reflection Layer at the bottom. The diagram illustrates a hierarchical structure, with data flowing through different layers of processing. The start node is the DataSyncAgent, and the end node is the Reflection Insights.

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram is divided into eight distinct layers, each representing a stage in the trading process:
1.  **Data Collection Layer**: Responsible for gathering market data.
2.  **Quant Analysis Layer**: Performs quantitative analysis on the collected data.
3.  **Prediction Layer**: Generates predictions based on the analyzed data.
4.  **Bull/Bear Adversarial Layer**: Provides bullish and bearish perspectives.
5.  **Reflection Layer**: Analyzes trade history and provides insights.
6.  **Decision Layer**: Makes trading decisions based on various inputs.
7.  **Risk Audit Layer**: Assesses the risk associated with the trading decisions.
8.  **Execution Layer**: Executes the trades.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **DataSyncAgent**: Responsible for fetching and synchronizing market data from various sources. This component likely interacts with external APIs or data feeds to retrieve real-time or historical market information.
- **LightGBM**: A gradient boosting framework used for prediction. The diagram indicates that the LightGBM model is automatically trained, suggesting an automated machine learning pipeline.
- **K-lines**: Also known as candlestick charts, are a type of financial chart that shows the price movement of an asset over time. Each candlestick represents a specific time period (e.g., 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour) and displays the opening, closing, high, and low prices.
- **Quant Signals**: Signals generated from quantitative analysis, used as input for decision-making.
- **RegimeDetector**: A component that identifies the current market regime (e.g., trending, ranging, volatile). This information is crucial for adapting trading strategies to the prevailing market conditions.
- **RiskAuditAgent**: A risk management component that assesses the risk associated with potential trades. This component likely considers factors such as volatility, position size, and market conditions to determine the overall risk level.
- **ExecutionEngine**: The component responsible for executing trades on the market. This component interacts with brokerage APIs or trading platforms to place orders and manage positions.
- **ReflectionAgent**: A component that analyzes past trades to identify patterns and improve future performance. This component likely uses machine learning techniques to extract insights from trade history.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The trading system begins with the `DataSyncAgent` collecting market data to create a `MarketSnapshot` containing K-lines at 5m, 15m, and 1h intervals. This `MarketSnapshot` serves as input for the `QuantAnalysisLayer`, the `PredictionLayer`, and the `Bull/Bear Adversarial Layer`. In the `QuantAnalysisLayer`, the `QuantAnalystAgent` utilizes `TrendSubAgent`, `OscillatorSubAgent`, and `SentimentSubAgent` to generate `Quant Signals`. This result is then passed to the `RiskAuditAgent` which generates an `AuditResult` with risk level and guard. The `ExecutionEngine` executes the trade, updating the `Trade History`. Finally, the `ReflectionAgent` analyzes the `Trade History` to generate `Reflection Insights`, completing the cycle. The system uses LightGBM for predictive modeling and incorporates risk assessment at the `RiskAuditLayer` before executing trades.
