## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the contract lifecycle process. It depicts the sequence of steps involved from the initial contract request by a customer, through validation, legal review, signature collection, and final archiving and distribution. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the contract workflow, aiding in understanding and optimizing the process.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) direction. It is hierarchically structured into stages representing different stakeholders and system actions. The process starts with the "Initiate Contract Request" node and ends with the "Send Contract to Stakeholders" node.

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram is divided into the following subgraphs, representing stages in the contract lifecycle:
- **Customer**: Actions initiated by the customer.
- **Legal Department**: Review and approval processes handled by the legal team.
- **System**: Automated notifications, signature recording, and contract status updates.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The contract request is sent for validation.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If contract details are valid, a pre-draft contract is generated.
- `B -- No --> Z`: If contract details are invalid, a validation rejection occurs.
- `C --> D`: The pre-draft contract is sent for legal review.
- `D --> E`: The legal department reviews the contract for approval.
- `E -- Yes --> F`: If the contract is approved, it is finalized.
- `E -- No --> C2`: If the contract is not approved, it is revised.
- `C2 --> D`: The revised pre-draft is sent back for legal review.
- `F --> G`: A notification is sent to request signatures.
- `G --> H`: The system checks if the customer has signed.
- `H -- Yes --> I`: If the customer has signed, their signature is recorded.
- `H -- No --> J`: If the customer has not signed, a reminder notification is sent.
- `J --> H`: The system loops back to check for the customer's signature again.
- `I --> K`: A notification is sent to request the legal department's signature.
- `K --> L`: The legal department signs the contract.
- `L --> M`: The legal department's signature is recorded.
- `M --> N`: The system checks if all signatures are present.
- `N -- Yes --> O`: If all signatures are present, the contract status is updated to "Signed".
- `N -- No --> Z2`: If all signatures are not present, a signature error is flagged.
- `O --> P`: The signed contract is archived as a PDF.
- `P --> Q`: The signed contract is sent to stakeholders.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Contract Request**: A formal request to initiate a contract.
- **Contract Details Validation**: The process of verifying the accuracy and completeness of the information provided in the contract request.
- **Pre-Draft Contract**: A preliminary version of the contract.
- **Legal Review**: Examination of the contract by legal professionals to ensure compliance and protect the organization's interests.
- **Finalize Contract Draft**: The process of completing and approving the final version of the contract.
- **Signing Notification**: An automated message sent to relevant parties to request their signatures on the contract.
- **Contract Status**: The current state of the contract (e.g., Draft, Signed, Archived).
- **Stakeholders**: Individuals or groups who have an interest in the contract.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The contract lifecycle process begins with the "Initiate Contract Request" node, where a customer submits a contract request. The process immediately proceeds to "Contract Details Validated?", a decision point determining whether the provided contract details are accurate and complete. If the details are valid ("Yes" branch), a "Generate Pre-Draft Contract" node is reached, creating a preliminary contract version. If the details are invalid ("No" branch), a "Validation Rejection" node is reached, terminating the process. The pre-draft contract then undergoes "Legal Review" by the legal department, which decides whether the contract is "Approved?". If approved, the contract is "Finalize Contract Draft"; otherwise, it is sent to "Revise Pre-Draft" and returns to the legal review stage. After finalization, a "Send Notification: Signing Required" node triggers notifications to relevant parties for signatures. The system checks "Customer Signed?" and resends notifications after a delay if needed. After the customer signs, the system proceeds to "Send Notification: Legal Signature Required", and the legal department signs the contract. The system then checks "All Signatures Present?". If yes, the "Update Contract Status: Signed" node is reached, followed by "Archive Contract PDF" and finally "Send Contract to Stakeholders". If not all signatures are present, a "Signature Error" node is reached. The "Contract Status" reflects the current state of the agreement, a key element in contract lifecycle management.
