## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Support Ticket**: A formal request for assistance or information from a customer.
- **Severity**: The level of impact or urgency associated with a support ticket.
- **Tier 2 Queue**: A support queue handled by more experienced or specialized support staff.
- **NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: A field of AI focused on enabling computers to understand and process human language.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: The process of determining the emotional tone or attitude expressed in a piece of text.
- **Sentiment Score**: A numerical value representing the positivity, negativity, or neutrality of the sentiment expressed in the text.
- **Proactive Support Team**: A support team that focuses on identifying and resolving potential issues before they impact customers.
- **Standard Support Queue**: The regular support queue where most customer issues are handled.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
