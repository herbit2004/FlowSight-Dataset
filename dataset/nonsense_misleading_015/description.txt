## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary

If payment is not received ("No" branch), a "Display Error Message" is shown, and the process loops back to "User Submits Order." If payment is received ("Yes" branch), an "Inventory Check" is performed. If the item is "Out of Stock," an "Email Customer" step is triggered, followed by a "Customer Confirms Cancellation?" decision. If the customer confirms cancellation ("Yes"), an "Issue Refund" step is executed, which then incorrectly leads to "Order Shipped." If the customer does not confirm cancellation ("No"), the item is "Backorder Item," which loops back to the "Inventory Check." If the "Inventory Check" finds the item "In Stock," the process moves to "Package Item," then "Shipping Label Printed," and finally "Order Shipped." Critically, the "Order Shipped" step then loops back to the "Payment Received?" step, creating an illogical flow where shipping precedes payment. The "Issue Refund" step also incorrectly converges into "Order Shipped." This diagram contains significant errors, including reversed sequences (shipping before payment), swapped branch outcomes (no email for in-stock items), and incorrect causal directions (cancellation confirmation before notification reason).
