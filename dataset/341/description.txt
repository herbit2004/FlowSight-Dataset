## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Symptom**: A subjective manifestation of a disease or condition reported by the patient.
- **Diagnosis**: The identification of a disease or condition by its signs and symptoms.
- **Medication**: A substance used to treat or prevent disease.
- **Treatment**: The management and care of a patient to combat a disease or disorder.
- **Side Effect**: An unintended effect of a medication.
- **CONTRAINDICATED_FOR**: Indicates that a medication should not be used in the presence of a specific symptom or condition.
- **TREATS**: Indicates that a medication or treatment is used to treat a specific diagnosis.
- **HAS_SYMPTOM**: Indicates that a diagnosis is associated with a specific symptom.
- **CAUSES**: Indicates that a medication causes a specific side effect.
