- `B -- Yes --> C`: If payment is received, acknowledge the order.
- `B -- No --> H`: If payment is not received, cancel the order.
- `C --> D`: Prepare the shipment after acknowledging the order.
- `D --> E`: Check if inventory is sufficient.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Receive Order**: Typically, this means the system has registered a new order, including customer details, items ordered, and payment information. In this diagram, it's the starting point without any prior confirmation of order details.
- **Payment Received?**: This usually involves verifying that the payment method is valid and the funds have been successfully transferred. In this diagram, it's a simple binary check without any error handling or retries.
- **Acknowledge Order**: Usually, this means sending a confirmation to the customer that their order has been received and is being processed. In this diagram, it happens even after an order is backordered, which is illogical.
- **Prepare Shipment**: This involves picking the items, packing them securely, and generating shipping labels. In this diagram, it happens after acknowledging the order.
- **Inventory Sufficient?**: This checks if the required quantity of each item in the order is available in the warehouse. In this diagram, a 'No' leads to backordering.
- **Ship Order**: This involves handing over the package to a shipping carrier. In this diagram, it leads directly to notifying the customer.
- **Order Backordered**: This means the order cannot be fulfilled immediately due to insufficient inventory and will be shipped later. In this diagram, it illogically loops back to acknowledging the order.
- **Customer Satisfied?**: This is a check to see if the customer is happy with the order and delivery. In this diagram, a 'Yes' leads to backordering the order.
- **Investigate Issue**: This involves looking into why the customer is not satisfied. In this diagram, it leads to issuing a refund.
- **Issue Refund**: This means returning the payment to the customer. In this diagram, it happens after investigating the issue.
- **Close Order**: This marks the order as complete in the system. In this diagram, it's the final step after shipping, canceling, backordering, or issuing a refund.
