The "Order Shipped" (F) also triggers "Send Shipment Confirmation" (I), creating a convergence. Finally, "Send Shipment Confirmation" (I) leads to "Order Delivered" (J), which then loops back to "User Initiates Order" (A), creating an illogical cycle.
