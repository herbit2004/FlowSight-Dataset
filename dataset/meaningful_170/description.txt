## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Trade Capture System**: A system responsible for capturing, validating, and storing trade data.
- **Payment System**: A system responsible for processing payments and handling settlement instructions.
- **Reconciliation Engine**: A system responsible for matching trade data with settlement data to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- **Trade Repository**: A database or storage system that stores trade data.
- **Settlement Instructions**: Instructions that specify how a payment should be made.
- **Reconciliation Logic**: The set of rules and algorithms used to match trade data with settlement data.
- **Operations Team**: A team responsible for handling exceptions and resolving issues.
- **Manual Investigation**: A process of manually reviewing data and processes to identify and resolve discrepancies.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with the Trade Capture System receiving trade data, which then undergoes validation. If the trade is valid, it's enriched before being stored in the Trade Repository; otherwise, it's rejected. Simultaneously, the Payment System receives settlement instructions and validates the details. Valid settlement details lead to payment execution, sending settlement data to the Reconciliation Engine, while invalid details result in payment rejection and logging of the failure. The Reconciliation Engine fetches trade data from the Trade Repository and settlement data from the Payment System, then applies reconciliation logic. If a match is found, a reconciliation report is generated, and the trade status is updated in the Trade Repository, completing the automated process. If no match is found, discrepancies are investigated, and the issue is escalated to the Operations Team for manual investigation. The results of the manual investigation are fed back into the reconciliation logic. The Trade Repository serves as a central data store, enabling the Reconciliation Engine to compare trade details against settlement information, a critical step in financial transaction processing. The end states are updating the trade status, escalating to operations, or logging a payment failure.
