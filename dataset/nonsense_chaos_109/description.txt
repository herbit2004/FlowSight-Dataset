## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The ChronoDrive sequence begins with an initiation step, immediately followed by a check for VeriCore compatibility. If VeriCore compatibility is confirmed, the sequence proceeds to trigger a SpectraQueue flush. If not, the system engages in a VaultNode handshake. A successful handshake also leads to the SpectraQueue flush, while a failed handshake initiates CoreSync validation. Passing CoreSync validation again leads to the SpectraQueue flush; failure triggers an attempt at ArcLane distributor routing. Successful ArcLane routing converges back to the SpectraQueue flush. If ArcLane routing fails, the system escalates the ZenithGate authentication layer and notifies the FluxBridge router of the failure, ultimately aborting the ChronoDrive sequence and logging the error to the AstroLog repository. After the SpectraQueue flush, TetraPulse modulation is performed, followed by DynoStream transmission. If the transmission is confirmed, the ChronoDrive transfer is completed, and the sequence ends successfully. If the transmission is not confirmed, PhaseShift recalibration is implemented. Successful recalibration loops back to DynoStream transmission, while unsuccessful recalibration logs an error to the AstroLog repository and aborts the ChronoDrive sequence. The sequence can end either with a successful ChronoDrive transfer or an aborted sequence due to various failures, with error logging to the AstroLog repository in the latter case.
