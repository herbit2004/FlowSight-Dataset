## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Node Type**: A classification of content elements within the system, determining their structure and behavior.
- **Inline Formatting**: Text formatting applied within a line of text, such as bold, italic, or links.
- **Top-Level Routable Entry Point**: A node that serves as a primary entry point for navigation within the system.
- **Annotated Text Property**: A text property that includes additional metadata or formatting information.
- **Block**: A content element that can contain other elements, such as images, text, or other blocks.
- **Text**: A content element consisting primarily of textual information.
