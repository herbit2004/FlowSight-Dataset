- `B -- "In Stock?" --> C`: If the items are in stock, the system proceeds to validate the credit card.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The flowchart begins with "Receive Order", which leads to an "Inventory Check". A positive "In Stock?" result incorrectly branches to both "Credit Card Valid?" and "Notify Customer: Out of Stock". If the "Credit Card Valid?" check returns "Yes", or even "No" (an error), the process proceeds to "Package Order". After packaging, the order is "Ship Order", which is then followed by "Update Inventory". The "Notify Customer: Out of Stock" node also leads to "Update Inventory". Critically, "Update Inventory" then loops back to "Receive Order", creating an illogical cycle. The diagram contains several errors: an "In Stock?" result leads to an out-of-stock notification, an invalid credit card leads to packaging, and the inventory update occurs before shipping and restarts the entire process. The standard e-commerce flow would involve payment capture after credit card validation and inventory update after shipping, neither of which are correctly represented here.
