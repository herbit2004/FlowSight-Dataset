## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram. It depicts the flow of an order processing system. The diagram outlines the steps involved in processing a customer order, from the initial placement of the order to its eventual closure or investigation of non-delivery.

## Overall Layout

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The customer order triggers the payment check.
- `B -- No --> C`: If payment is not received, send order confirmation.
- `B -- Yes --> D`: If payment is received, update inventory.
- `D --> E`: Inventory update leads to checking inventory sufficiency.
- `E -- No --> F`: If inventory is not sufficient, send an 'Out of Stock' notification.
- `E -- Yes --> H`: If inventory is sufficient, pack the order.
- `F --> G`: 'Out of Stock' notification leads to order cancellation.
- `H --> I`: Packing the order leads to shipping the order.
- `I --> J`: Shipping the order leads to generating an invoice.
- `J --> K`: Invoice generation leads to checking if the customer received the order.
- `K -- Yes --> L`: If the customer received the order, close the order.
- `K -- No --> M`: If the customer did not receive the order, investigate non-delivery.
- `M --> D`: Investigating non-delivery leads back to updating inventory.
- `G --> L`: Order cancellation leads to closing the order.
- `C --> G`: Sending order confirmation leads to order cancellation.
