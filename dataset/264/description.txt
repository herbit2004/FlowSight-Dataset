## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the process of determining funding sources for a project, specifically focusing on whether to pursue a Grants Program or Treasury funding. The diagram helps to guide the decision-making process based on the project's focus and stage of development. It is used to determine the appropriate funding path.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out from left to right (LR). It starts with the "Project Focus" node and branches out based on the project's nature and development stage, ultimately leading to either a "Grants Program" or "Treasury or Grants Program" funding source.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
  `Project Focus --> Stage of Development`: Indicates that the project's focus leads to determining its stage of development.
  `Project Focus --> Grants Program`: Indicates that the project's focus leads to Grants Program.
  `Project Focus --> Treasury`: Indicates that the project's focus leads to Treasury.
  `Stage of Development -- Existing POC --> Goal of the Application`: If there is an existing Proof of Concept (POC), the next step is to define the goal of the application.
  `Stage of Development -- No POC --> Grants Program`: If there is no existing Proof of Concept (POC), the project should consider the Grants Program.
  `Goal of the Application --> Treasury or Grants Program`: After defining the goal of the application, the project can pursue funding through either the Treasury or a Grants Program.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Grants Program**: A funding mechanism where funds are awarded to projects based on specific criteria. The diagram links to the W3F Grants Program process.
- **Treasury**: A funding mechanism where funds are allocated from a central treasury. The diagram links to the Polkadot Network Treasury.
- **POC**: Proof of Concept, a preliminary study to demonstrate the feasibility of a project.
- **Development**: The stage of building and implementing the project.
- **Research**: The stage of investigating and exploring the project.
- **Funding**: The process of securing financial resources for the project.
