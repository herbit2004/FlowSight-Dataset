## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Initial data flows to the CoreSync Validation Check.
- `B -- Validation Passed --> C`: Data passes validation and proceeds to FluxBridge Router Assignment.
- `B -- Validation Failed --> D`: Data fails validation and proceeds to VaultNode Handshake Attempt.
- `D -- Handshake Successful --> E`: Handshake is successful, proceeds to ArcLane Distributor Allocation.
- `D -- Handshake Failed --> F`: Handshake fails, proceeds to SpectraQueue Flush Protocol.
- `F --> G`: SpectraQueue Flush Protocol leads to ZenithGate Auth Layer Bypass.
- `G --> C`: ZenithGate Auth Layer Bypass converges back to FluxBridge Router Assignment.
- `C --> H`: FluxBridge Router Assignment leads to DeltaStream Integrity Assessor.
- `H -- Integrity OK --> I`: Data integrity is OK, proceeds to PrecisionCluster Dispatch.
- `H -- Integrity Failure --> J`: Data integrity fails, proceeds to ChrononBuffer Rollback Initiation.
- `I --> K`: PrecisionCluster Dispatch leads to VertexWeave Synchronization.
- `K --> L`: VertexWeave Synchronization leads to EchoPulse Resonance Test.
- `L -- Resonance Stable --> M`: Resonance is stable, proceeds to InfraMatrix Transmogrification.
- `L -- Resonance Unstable --> N`: Resonance is unstable, proceeds to ParallaxEngine Redirection.
- `M --> O`: InfraMatrix Transmogrification leads to RippleDrive Stabilization.
- `O --> P`: RippleDrive Stabilization leads to CoreMetric Abstraction Layer.
- `P --> Q`: CoreMetric Abstraction Layer leads to TerraForm Gradient Analyzer.
- `Q -- Gradient Acceptable --> R`: Gradient is acceptable, proceeds to HyperPhase Commit Sequence.
- `Q -- Gradient Unstable --> N`: Gradient is unstable, proceeds to ParallaxEngine Redirection.
- `R --> S`: HyperPhase Commit Sequence leads to QuantumLattice Consolidation.
- `S --> T`: QuantumLattice Consolidation leads to Final CoreSync Verification.
- `T -- Verification Passed --> U`: Verification passes, proceeds to End: Complete Data Propagation.
- `T -- Verification Failed --> J`: Verification fails, proceeds to ChrononBuffer Rollback Initiation.
- `N --> I`: ParallaxEngine Redirection converges back to PrecisionCluster Dispatch.
- `J --> A`: ChrononBuffer Rollback Initiation loops back to Start: Initial Data Intake.
