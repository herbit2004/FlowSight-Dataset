## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The CoreFlux Ingress process begins by initiating the ingress and validating the MetaFrame signature. If the signature is valid, the system attempts to acquire a NexusGuard lease; otherwise, an alert is dispatched to the ZenithGate Auth Layer, and an incident is logged in the ShadowTrace Ledger before proceeding to finalize the CoreFlux transmit. If the NexusGuard lease is granted, the ArcLane Distributor is engaged, and the payload is routed via the FluxBridge Router, then passed to SpectraQueue Flush. A key decision point is whether the SpectraQueue flush is successful. If successful, a VaultNode Integrity Check is performed; otherwise, the DeltaSync Recovery Protocol is triggered, activating the TemporalRewind Buffer before finalizing the transmit. Another branch occurs after the VaultNode Integrity Check: if the VaultNode integrity is OK, CoreSync Validation is initiated; otherwise, the DeltaSync Recovery Protocol is triggered. The ZenithGate Auth Layer and ShadowTrace Ledger are used for security and auditing. Finally, after either CoreSync Validation or TemporalRewind Buffer activation, the CoreFlux transmit is finalized, the ingress completion is acknowledged, and the process ends.
