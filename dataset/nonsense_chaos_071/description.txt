## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Starts the VectraCore Ingestion process.
- `B -- Pass --> C`: DataFlux Integrity Check passes, proceeds to ArcFlow Transformation.
- `B -- Fail --> E`: DataFlux Integrity Check fails, triggers QuantaLog Event Alert.
- `C --> D`: Proceeds from ArcFlow Transformation to ChronoStream Validation.
- `D -- Valid --> F`: ChronoStream Validation passes, proceeds to SynapsePipe Routing.
- `D -- Invalid --> E`: ChronoStream Validation fails, triggers QuantaLog Event Alert.
- `E --> G`: QuantaLog Event Alert triggers VectraCore Reject alert.
- `G --> H`: VectraCore Reject alert leads to VectraCore Failure end.
- `F --> I`: Proceeds from SynapsePipe Routing to ZenithGate Auth Layer.
- `I -- Authenticated --> J`: ZenithGate Auth Layer authenticates, proceeds to CoreSync Dispatch.
- `I -- Unauthenticated --> E`: ZenithGate Auth Layer fails to authenticate, triggers QuantaLog Event Alert.
- `J --> K`: Proceeds from CoreSync Dispatch to FluxBridge Router.
- `K -- Primary --> L`: FluxBridge Router routes to VaultNode Handshake (primary path).
- `K -- Secondary --> M`: FluxBridge Router routes to ResonanceBuffer Hold (secondary path).
- `L --> N`: VaultNode Handshake proceeds to SpectraQueue Flush.
- `M --> N`: ResonanceBuffer Hold proceeds to SpectraQueue Flush.
- `L, M --> N`: Paths from VaultNode Handshake and ResonanceBuffer Hold converge at SpectraQueue Flush.
- `N --> O`: Proceeds from SpectraQueue Flush to ArcLane Distributor.
- `O --> P`: Proceeds from ArcLane Distributor to VectraCore Delivery end.
