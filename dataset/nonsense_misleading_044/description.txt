## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating an order processing workflow. It depicts the steps involved in processing a user's order, from submission to completion, including inventory checks, payment processing, shipping, and delivery. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the order fulfillment process, but contains intentional errors.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's layout is top-down (TD). The flow starts with "User submits order" at the top and progresses downwards through various decision points and actions, ultimately leading to "Order complete" or "Notify user: Order failed".
