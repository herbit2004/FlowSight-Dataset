## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Starts the CoreSync process and initiates the VaultNode handshake.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: VaultNode handshake is successful, proceeds to ZenithGate authentication.
- `B -- No --> D`: VaultNode handshake fails, proceeds to ArcLane distribution.
- `C --> E`: Proceeds to check if SpectraQueue flush is complete after ZenithGate authentication.
- `E -- Yes --> F`: SpectraQueue flush is complete, engages the FluxBridge router.
- `E -- No --> G`: SpectraQueue flush is not complete, performs DeltaResonance recalibration.
- `G --> E`: After DeltaResonance recalibration, returns to check SpectraQueue flush again.
- `F --> H`: After FluxBridge router engagement, processes data through ProtoCache buffer.
- `H --> I`: Proceeds to check for transient overflow after data processing.
- `I -- Yes --> J`: Transient overflow is detected, activates TemporalStabilization Protocol.
- `I -- No --> K`: Transient overflow is not detected, routes to MetaFlow Aggregator.
- `J --> K`: After activating TemporalStabilization Protocol, routes to MetaFlow Aggregator.
- `K --> L`: Routes data to CoreSync Validation from MetaFlow Aggregator.
- `D --> Q`: Proceeds to begin cross-thread alignment after ArcLane distribution.
- `Q --> R`: Applies dynamic vector rescaling after cross-thread alignment.
- `R --> L`: Routes data to CoreSync Validation after dynamic vector rescaling.
- `L --> M`: Proceeds to check if CoreSync validation is successful.
- `M -- Yes --> N`: CoreSync validation is successful, ends CoreSync completion.
- `M -- No --> O`: CoreSync validation fails, initiates recursive fault isolation.
- `O --> P`: Ends CoreSync failure with data preservation after fault isolation.

The CoreSync Initiation process begins with the "Start: CoreSync Initiation" node, immediately followed by a "VaultNode Handshake?" check. If the handshake is successful ("Yes" branch), the process proceeds to the "ZenithGate Auth Layer" for authentication. Otherwise ("No" branch), it diverts to "ArcLane Distributor" and then "Begin CrossThread Alignment" followed by "Apply DynamicVector Rescaling". After ZenithGate authentication, the process checks if "SpectraQueue Flush Complete?". If "Yes", the "FluxBridge Router" is engaged, and data is processed through the "ProtoCache Buffer". Post-ProtoCache buffering, a "TransientOverflow Detected?" check determines whether to activate the "TemporalStabilization Protocol". Regardless of overflow detection, the process routes to the "MetaFlow Aggregator". Both the MetaFlow route and the DynamicVector Rescaling converge at "CoreSync Validation". Finally, a "CoreSync Validation Successful?" check determines the outcome: "End: CoreSync Completion" if successful, or "Initiate Recursive FaultIsolation" leading to "End: CoreSync Failure - Data Preservation" if unsuccessful. The VaultNode handshake and ZenithGate authentication suggest a security-conscious design, while the SpectraQueue and ProtoCache indicate data buffering and processing stages within the synchronization flow.
