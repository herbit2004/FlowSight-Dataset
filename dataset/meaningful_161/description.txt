## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram depicting an order processing workflow. It illustrates the sequence of steps involved in processing an order, from the initial receipt of the order to its completion or cancellation. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the order processing logic, aiding in understanding and optimizing the process.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) direction. It consists of two subgraphs: "Order Processing Workflow" and "Reporting & Alerts". The start node is "Order Received", and the end nodes are "Order Completion", "Generate Shipment Report", "Generate Cancellation Report", and "Trigger Low Stock Alert".

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram contains two explicit subgraphs:
- **Order Processing Workflow**: This subgraph encompasses the core steps of processing an order, including inventory checks, payment processing, shipment initiation, and order fulfillment.
- **Reporting & Alerts**: This subgraph focuses on generating reports and alerts related to order processing, such as shipment reports, cancellation reports, and low stock alerts.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: Order is received, initiating the inventory check.
- `B -- Available --> C`: Inventory is available, allocate inventory.
- `B -- Unavailable --> E`: Inventory is unavailable, backorder item.
- `E --> F`: Await restock of the backordered item.
- `F --> C`: After restock, allocate inventory.
- `C --> D`: Inventory is allocated, await payment.
- `D -- Payment Received --> G`: Payment is received, initiate shipment.
- `D -- Payment Failed --> H`: Payment failed, order cancellation.
- `G --> I`: Initiate shipment leads to generating a shipping label.
- `I --> J`: Generate shipping label leads to package order.
- `J --> K`: Package order leads to hand off to courier.
- `K --> L`: Hand off to courier leads to updating order status to shipped.
- `H --> N`: Order cancellation leads to releasing inventory.
- `L --> M`: Order status shipped leads to order completion.
- `N --> O`: Release inventory leads to updating order status to cancelled.
- `O --> M`: Order status cancelled leads to order completion.
- `L --> P`: Update order status: Shipped triggers generating a shipment report.
- `H --> Q`: Order cancellation triggers generating a cancellation report.
- `B -- Low Stock --> R`: Inventory check reveals low stock, triggering a low stock alert.
- Paths `L` and `O` converge at `M`.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Order Processing Workflow**: The sequence of steps involved in fulfilling a customer's order, from placement to delivery.
- **Inventory Check**: Verifying the availability of products in the warehouse or stock system.
- **Allocate Inventory**: Reserving specific units of a product for a pending order.
- **Backorder**: An order for a product that is temporarily out of stock.
- **Restock**: Replenishing the inventory with new units of a product.
- **Payment Gateway**: A service that processes online payments for e-commerce transactions.
- **Shipping Label**: A document containing information for shipping a package, including addresses and tracking numbers.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The order processing workflow begins with "Order Received," which triggers an "Inventory Check." If the item is "Available," the workflow proceeds to "Allocate Inventory," then "Await Payment." If the item is "Unavailable," it goes to "Backorder Item" and "Await Restock" before returning to "Allocate Inventory." Upon "Payment Received," the workflow proceeds to "Initiate Shipment," "Generate Shipping Label," "Package Order," and "Hand Off to Courier," culminating in "Update Order Status: Shipped." A failed payment leads to "Order Cancellation" and "Release Inventory," followed by "Update Order Status: Cancelled." Both "Update Order Status: Shipped" and "Update Order Status: Cancelled" lead to "Order Completion." The "Inventory Check" step can also trigger a "Low Stock" alert. "Update Order Status: Shipped" triggers "Generate Shipment Report," while "Order Cancellation" triggers "Generate Cancellation Report." These reports, along with the "Trigger Low Stock Alert," fall under the "Reporting & Alerts" subgraph. The "Inventory Check" step is crucial for determining whether an order can be fulfilled immediately or requires a backorder, directly impacting customer satisfaction and order fulfillment time.
