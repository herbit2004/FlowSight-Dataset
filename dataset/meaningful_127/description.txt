## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Campaign Input**: The data used to target and personalize campaigns, including user demographics, behavior, and preferences.
- **Rule Engine**: A system that applies predefined rules to determine user eligibility for a campaign.
- **Eligibility Check**: The process of determining whether a user meets the criteria to participate in a campaign.
- **Personalization Logic**: The algorithms and processes used to tailor content and offers to individual users.
- **Dynamic Content Selection**: The process of choosing the most relevant content for a user based on their profile and behavior.
- **A/B Test Split**: Dividing users into two or more groups to test different versions of content or offers.
- **Content Delivery**: The process of delivering the appropriate content to the targeted users.
- **Tracking**: Monitoring user engagement metrics to measure the effectiveness of a campaign.
- **Impression**: A metric representing each time an ad or content is displayed.
- **Click**: A metric representing each time a user clicks on an ad or content.
- **Conversion**: A metric representing a desired action taken by a user, such as a purchase or sign-up.
