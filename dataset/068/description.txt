## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Azure Data Factory (ADF)**: A cloud-based data integration service that allows you to create data-driven workflows for orchestrating data movement and transforming data at scale.
- **Pipeline**: A logical grouping of activities that performs a unit of work. Activities in a pipeline define actions to perform on your data.
- **Data Flow**: A visually designed data transformation process within ADF, allowing for complex data transformations without coding.
- **Dataset**: Represents data storage within ADF, defining the structure, location, and format of the data.
- **Linked Service**: Defines the connection information needed for ADF to connect to external resources, such as databases or storage accounts.
- **Integration Runtime**: The compute infrastructure used by ADF to execute data flows and activities.
- **Trigger**: Represents the event or schedule that starts a pipeline execution.
