If payment is not received ("No" branch), an "Order Confirmation" is sent, and the process proceeds to "Close Order," looping back to "User Submits Order." If the "Customer Accepts Backorder?", the process moves to "Fulfill Order"; otherwise, the "Order" is "Cancelled" and a "Refund" is issued, followed by "Update Inventory" and "Close Order." If the "Inventory Check" indicates items are "Available," the process proceeds to "Fulfill Order," then "Packing and Shipping." The "Update Inventory" step precedes "Close Order," which then erroneously loops back to "User Submits Order," restarting the entire process. The "Send Order Confirmation" step occurs prematurely, before payment verification, which is a deviation from standard order processing.
