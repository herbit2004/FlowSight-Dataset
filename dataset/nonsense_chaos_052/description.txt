## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: The start trigger initiates the VeridianCore checkpoint check.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If the VeridianCore checkpoint exists, the data is sent to the DeltaWave Preprocessor.
- `B -- No --> D`: If the VeridianCore checkpoint does not exist, the data is sent to the EpsilonVault Retrieval.
- `C --> E`: The DeltaWave Preprocessor sends its output to the ZetaStream Regulator.
- `D --> E`: The EpsilonVault Retrieval sends its output to the ZetaStream Regulator.
- `E --> F`: The ZetaStream Regulator sends its output to the GammaField Alignment Success? decision point.
- `F -- Yes --> G`: If the GammaField alignment is successful, the data is sent to the ThetaLink Transponder.
- `F -- No --> H`: If the GammaField alignment is not successful, the data is sent to the LambdaCycle Reprocessing.
- `H --> I`: The LambdaCycle Reprocessing sends its output to the KappaMesh Scrubbing.
- `I --> F`: The KappaMesh Scrubbing sends its output back to the GammaField Alignment Success? decision point, creating a loop.
- `G --> J`: The ThetaLink Transponder sends its output to the IotaSphere Partitioning.
- `J --> K`: The IotaSphere Partitioning sends its output to the MuVector Confirmation? decision point.
- `K -- Yes --> L`: If the MuVector is confirmed, the data is sent to the XiBlade Executor.
- `K -- No --> M`: If the MuVector is not confirmed, the data is sent to the RhoChain Routing.
- `M --> J`: The RhoChain Routing sends its output back to the IotaSphere Partitioning, creating a loop.
- `L --> N`: The XiBlade Executor sends its output to the End: SigmaPulse Delivery.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The data processing pipeline begins with the `CeruleanSource Trigger`, which initiates the workflow. The first decision point is the `VeridianCore Checkpoint?`. If the checkpoint exists (`Yes` branch), the data is preprocessed by the `DeltaWave Preprocessor`; otherwise (`No` branch), the `EpsilonVault Retrieval` is used. Both paths converge at the `ZetaStream Regulator`, which then feeds into the `GammaField Alignment Success?` decision. If alignment is successful (`Yes` branch), the data is processed by the `ThetaLink Transponder` and then `IotaSphere Partitioning`. If alignment fails (`No` branch), `LambdaCycle Reprocessing` and `KappaMesh Scrubbing` are performed, looping back to the `GammaField Alignment Success?` check. After `IotaSphere Partitioning`, the `MuVector Confirmation?` determines the final path. If confirmed (`Yes` branch), the `XiBlade Executor` is used, leading to the `End: SigmaPulse Delivery`. If not confirmed (`No` branch), `RhoChain Routing` loops back to `IotaSphere Partitioning`. The `VeridianCore Checkpoint` acts as an initial branching point, determining the preprocessing or retrieval method, while the `GammaField Alignment` and `MuVector Confirmation` steps control iterative reprocessing and routing, respectively.
