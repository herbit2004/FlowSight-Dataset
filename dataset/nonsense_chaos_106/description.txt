## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with the `EpochGenesis Initiator`, which triggers the `PreFlux Integrity Check?`. If the PreFlux integrity is confirmed (`Yes`), the flow proceeds to `CoreSync Validation`; otherwise (`No`), it goes to `VaultNode Handshake`. Both `CoreSync Validation` and `VaultNode Handshake` converge at `Transversal Buffer Allocation`. Next, the `LumenField Confirmation?` determines whether to proceed to `ArcLane Distributor` (`Yes`) or `SpectraQueue Flush` (`No`), both of which converge at `ZenithGate Auth Layer`. The flow continues through `FluxBridge Router` to `Temporal Drift Detected?`. If drift is detected (`Yes`), `DeltaWave Recalibration` is performed; otherwise (`No`), the process moves directly to `Parallel Gradient Descent`. `DeltaWave Recalibration` also leads into `Parallel Gradient Descent`, which then proceeds to `CoreMatrix Alignment`. The `QuantumState Stable?` check determines whether to propagate the `EventHorizon Propagation` (`Yes`) or reset the `InertiaDampener Reset` (`No`). Both paths converge at `ProtonShell Consolidation`, ultimately leading to the `End: TemporalConvergence Achieved` state. The `PreFlux Integrity Check` is a critical early decision point, influencing whether a secure `VaultNode Handshake` is required, highlighting the system's focus on data integrity and secure communication protocols.
