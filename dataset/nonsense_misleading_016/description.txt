## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram depicting the process of handling a customer order. It outlines the steps involved from receiving the order to its completion, including inventory checks, payment processing, shipping, and post-delivery actions. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the order fulfillment workflow.

## Overall Layout
The diagram has a top-down (TD) layout. The process starts with "Receive Customer Order" at the top and progresses downwards towards "Complete Order" at the bottom. The diagram includes decision points that create branching paths.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `A --> B`: An order is sent for inventory check.
- `B -- Yes --> C`: If the inventory check is successful (items are in stock), proceed to payment processing.
- `B -- No --> F`: If the inventory check fails (items are out of stock), cancel the order.
- `C --> D`: Payment is sent for success check.
- `D -- No --> C`: If the payment is unsuccessful, loop back to payment processing.
- `D -- Yes --> E`: If the payment is successful, send a shipping notification.
- `E --> A`: Shipping Notification incorrectly loops back to Receive Customer Order.
- `F --> G`: If the order is cancelled, notify the customer.
- `G --> H`: After notifying the customer, log the cancellation reason.
- `H --> I`: After logging the cancellation reason, update the inventory.
- `I --> J`: After updating the inventory, send the order to the warehouse.
- `J --> K`: The order is sent to be packed.
- `K --> L`: The packed order is shipped.
- `L --> M`: The order is delivered.
- `M --> N`: After delivery, send a post-delivery survey.
- `N --> O`: After sending the survey, complete the order.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Receive Customer Order**: The initial action of receiving an order from a customer. In this diagram, it's the starting point of the entire process.
- **Inventory Check**: Verifying the availability of items in stock. In this diagram, it's a decision point that determines whether the order can proceed or needs to be cancelled.
- **Payment Processing**: The process of charging the customer for the order. In this diagram, it's a step that follows a successful inventory check.
- **Shipping Notification**: A notification sent to the customer confirming that their order has been shipped. In this diagram, it's triggered after successful payment, but incorrectly loops back to the start.

- **Send Order to Warehouse**: Transmitting the order details to the warehouse for fulfillment. In this diagram, this happens after inventory is updated (following a cancellation).
- **Pack Order**: Preparing the order for shipment at the warehouse. In this diagram, it follows sending the order to the warehouse.
- **Ship Order**: Sending the order to the customer. In this diagram, it follows packing the order.
- **Order Delivered**: Confirmation that the order has been delivered to the customer. In this diagram, it follows shipping the order.
- **Send Post-Delivery Survey**: Sending a survey to the customer after the order has been delivered. In this diagram, this happens *before* the order is completed.
- **Complete Order**: The final step in the order fulfillment process. In this diagram, it follows sending the post-delivery survey.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary

If the inventory check fails ("No" branch), the order is "Cancel Order," and the customer is notified, the cancellation reason is logged, and then the inventory is updated. After "Payment Processing," the diagram checks "Payment Successful?". If payment fails ("No"), the process loops back to "Payment Processing." If payment is successful ("Yes"), a "Shipping Notification" is sent, which incorrectly loops back to "Receive Customer Order." If the order is cancelled, the process continues with "Notify Customer of Cancellation," followed by "Log Cancellation Reason," and then "Update Inventory." After updating inventory, the process proceeds to "Send Order to Warehouse," then "Pack Order," "Ship Order," and "Order Delivered." Following delivery, a "Send Post-Delivery Survey" is sent, and then the process ends with "Complete Order." The "Update Inventory" step is misplaced, occurring after cancellation but before sending the order to the warehouse. The shipping notification incorrectly loops back to the start. The post-delivery survey happens before the order is completed.
