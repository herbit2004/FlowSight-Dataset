## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Fast Pair**: A Google-developed Bluetooth technology that enables quick and easy pairing of Bluetooth devices.
- **Vulnerability**: A weakness in a system that can be exploited to cause harm.
- **Exploit**: A piece of code or a technique that takes advantage of a vulnerability to cause unintended or unanticipated behavior.
- **Patched**: The process of fixing a vulnerability.
- **HFP (Hands-Free Profile)**: A Bluetooth profile that allows car kits and other hands-free devices to communicate with mobile phones.
- **Microphone Access**: The ability to use the microphone of a device, often a target of exploitation.
