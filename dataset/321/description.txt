## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the features of the Prisma Zod Generator (PZG), differentiating between core (free) features and pro (paid) features. It serves as a feature map, outlining the capabilities of the generator and how they are organized into different categories. The diagram helps users understand the scope of the tool and the benefits of upgrading to the pro version.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out from left to right, with two main branches representing "PRO FEATURES" and "CORE FEATURES". Each branch expands into categories and specific features, forming a hierarchical structure. The start nodes are "PZG Pro" and "Prisma Zod Generator", and the end nodes are the individual features listed at the rightmost part of the diagram.

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram contains two explicit subgraphs:
- **CORE FEATURES (Free Forever)**: Encompasses the basic functionalities of the Prisma Zod Generator, available for free.
- **PRO FEATURES (Paid Plans)**: Includes advanced features that require a paid subscription to access.
