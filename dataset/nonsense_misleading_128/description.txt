## Edges, Branches & Convergence
`A --> B`: An order is received and proceeds to payment processing.
`B -- No --> C`: If payment is not successful, the order is canceled.
`B -- Yes --> D`: If payment is successful, a shipping label is printed.
`D --> E`: After printing the shipping label, the item is picked and packed.
`E --> F`: After picking and packing, the system checks if the shipping confirmation was sent.
`F -- No --> G`: If the shipping confirmation was not sent, the process returns to "Item Picked & Packed".
`F -- Yes --> H`: If the shipping confirmation was sent, the item is marked as delivered.
`H --> A`: After item is delivered, the process loops back to "Order Received".
`E --> I`: After picking and packing, the inventory is updated.
`I --> C`: After updating inventory, the order is canceled.
`C --> J`: After canceling the order, a refund is processed.
`J --> K`: After processing the refund, the order is completed.
`H --> K`: After the item is delivered, the order is completed.
