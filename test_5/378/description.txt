This data then undergoes normalization and cleaning in the `CLEAN` node, followed by feature extraction in the `FEAT` node, and time-window slicing in the `WINDOW` node. The pipeline's effectiveness hinges on the quality of data ingested and the efficacy of the model training and evaluation processes.
