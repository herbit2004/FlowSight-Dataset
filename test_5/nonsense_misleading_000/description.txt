## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the incident management process. It depicts the flow of an incident from submission to resolution, involving support and engineering teams. The diagram outlines the steps taken to address and resolve incidents, including severity determination, assignment, escalation, and deployment of fixes.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's layout is top-down (TD). It starts with the "User Submits Incident Report" node at the top and flows downwards through decision points and actions. The diagram ends with the "Incident Marked as Resolved" node, which incorrectly loops back to the start.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Incident Report**: A formal record of an issue or problem encountered by a user. In this diagram, it's the starting point of the workflow.
- **Severity**: The level of impact or urgency of an incident. In this diagram, if severity cannot be determined, the incident is immediately closed, which is an error.
- **Support Team**: The team responsible for initial triage and resolution of incidents. In this diagram, they attempt to resolve the incident after it's assigned.
- **Engineering Team**: The team responsible for resolving more complex incidents that require code changes or infrastructure modifications. In this diagram, incidents are escalated to engineering if the support team cannot resolve them.
- **Fix**: A solution or patch that resolves an incident. In this diagram, the fix is deployed to production after either the support or engineering team resolves the incident.
- **Production**: The live environment where the application or service is running. In this diagram, the fix is deployed to the production environment.
- **Resolution**: The act of resolving an incident. In this diagram, the user is notified of the resolution, and the incident is marked as resolved.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary

If the severity cannot be determined (B -- No --> H), the incident is immediately closed, which is an error in the flow. If the severity is determined (B -- Yes --> C), the incident is assigned to the support team. The support team then attempts to resolve the incident (D). If support cannot resolve the incident (D -- No --> E), it is escalated to the engineering team.
