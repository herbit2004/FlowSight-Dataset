## Diagram Type & Purpose

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Inventory Service**: A service responsible for managing and tracking inventory levels.
- **Deduct Request**: A request to reduce the quantity of an item in the inventory.
- **Pre-Deduction Checks**: Validations performed before deducting inventory, such as checking for sufficient stock.
- **Lock Inventory Record**: A mechanism to prevent concurrent modifications to the inventory record, ensuring data consistency.
- **Deduct Inventory Qty**: The process of reducing the quantity of an item in the inventory database.
- **Message Queue**: A communication system that allows different services to exchange messages asynchronously.
- **Rollback Process**: A procedure to revert changes made to the inventory in case of a failure.
- **Rollback Handler**: A component responsible for executing the rollback process.
- **InventoryDeductedEvent**: An event published to indicate a successful inventory deduction.
- **InventoryDeductionFailedEvent**: An event published to indicate a failed inventory deduction.
- **InventoryRollbackRequest**: A request to initiate the rollback process for a specific inventory deduction.
- **InventoryRollbackCompletedEvent**: An event published to indicate the successful completion of the inventory rollback process.
