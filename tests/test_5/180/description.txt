## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Dynamic Selling Engine**: The core component responsible for managing the token selling process based on market conditions and predefined strategies.
- **BullRun**: A market condition characterized by a sustained period of rising prices.
- **BearTrend**: A market condition characterized by a sustained period of falling prices.
- **Sideways Market**: A market condition where prices fluctuate within a narrow range, showing no clear upward or downward trend.
- **High Volatility**: A market condition characterized by large and rapid price fluctuations.
- **Trailing Stop**: A stop-loss order that adjusts automatically as the price of the asset increases, locking in profits while limiting potential losses.
- **Stop Loss**: An order placed with a broker to buy or sell when the asset reaches a certain price.
- **Take Profit**: An order placed with a broker to buy or sell when the asset reaches a certain price, ensuring a profit.
