## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Planner Agent**: An agent responsible for creating a plan for the research report, including defining the sections and background context.
- **Iterative Researcher**: A component that iteratively researches and drafts a specific section of the report.
- **Proofreader Agent**: An agent responsible for proofreading and finalizing the drafts of the report sections.
- **Parallel Section Research**: A stage where multiple sections of the report are researched and drafted in parallel.
- **Query**: The initial question or topic that the research report aims to address.
- **max_iterations**: The maximum number of iterations allowed for the iterative research process.
- **max_time**: The maximum time allowed for the entire research report generation process.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with "User Input," which includes the research query, maximum iterations, and maximum time constraints. This input is fed into the "Planner Agent," which generates a "Report plan" containing the sections and background context for the research report. The report plan then initiates the "Parallel Section Research" stage, where "IterativeResearcher" agents work concurrently on different sections (1, 2, and 3). Each IterativeResearcher produces a "Section Draft." These drafts then converge at the "Proofreader Agent," which integrates and refines them into the "Final Research Report." The "Planner Agent" is responsible for structuring the research by defining sections, enabling parallel research. The iterative researchers then work within the constraints of the plan to produce the individual sections. The final report is the culmination of the parallel research and proofreading stages.
