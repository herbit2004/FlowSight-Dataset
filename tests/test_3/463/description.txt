## Repository Context & Terminology
- **C# events**: A mechanism in C# for implementing the observer pattern, allowing objects to subscribe to and be notified of events.
- **Direct references**: Direct calls between objects or systems, creating tight coupling.
- **DxMessaging**: A messaging library or framework (likely C# based, given the context) that provides features like observability, decoupling, and lifecycle management for inter-system communication. Further context would be needed to determine the specific implementation.
- **Observable messaging**: Messaging that allows subscribers to observe the flow of messages and react accordingly.
- **Decoupled messaging**: Messaging that reduces dependencies between systems, allowing them to evolve independently.
- **Lifecycle-safe messaging**: Messaging that handles the lifecycle of systems and ensures that messages are delivered correctly even when systems are starting, stopping, or failing.
