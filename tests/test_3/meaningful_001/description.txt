## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram illustrating the order processing workflow in an e-commerce system. It depicts the sequence of steps from the initial user action of placing an order to the final logging of order and transaction data. The diagram serves as a visual representation of the order processing logic, aiding in understanding and potentially optimizing the system.

## Overall Layout
The diagram's primary direction is top-to-bottom (TD). It is hierarchically structured into four subgraphs representing stages of the order process. The start node is "User Action: Initiate Order," and the end node is "Log Order & Transaction Data."

## Subgraphs / Stages
The diagram is divided into the following subgraphs:
- **Order Creation**: Initial stage involving product availability check and order instance creation.
- **Payment Processing**: Handles payment method selection, validation, and fund capture.
- **Order Fulfillment**: Focuses on updating order status, inventory management, and shipping label generation.
- **Post-Processing**: Includes sending order confirmation emails and logging transaction data.

## Edges, Branches & Convergence
- `User Action: Initiate Order --> Product Availability Check`: The user's action triggers a product availability check.
- `Product Availability Check -- Available --> Create Order Instance`: If the product is available, an order instance is created.
- `Product Availability Check -- Unavailable --> Display Out-of-Stock Message`: If the product is unavailable, an out-of-stock message is displayed.
- `Create Order Instance --> Apply Promotions/Discounts`: After creating the order instance, promotions and discounts are applied.
- `Apply Promotions/Discounts --> Calculate Order Total`: After applying promotions and discounts, the order total is calculated.
- `Calculate Order Total --> Select Payment Method`: After calculating the order total, the user selects a payment method.
- `Select Payment Method -- Credit Card --> CC Validation & Auth via Payment Gateway`: If the user selects credit card, the system validates and authorizes the payment.
- `Select Payment Method -- PayPal --> Redirect to PayPal for Login`: If the user selects PayPal, the system redirects to PayPal for login.
- `CC Validation & Auth via Payment Gateway -- Approved --> Capture Funds from CC`: If the credit card payment is approved, the funds are captured.
- `CC Validation & Auth via Payment Gateway -- Declined --> Display Payment Error`: If the credit card payment is declined, a payment error is displayed.
- `Redirect to PayPal for Login --> PayPal Authorization Obtained`: After successful PayPal login and authorization, PayPal authorization is obtained.
- `PayPal Authorization Obtained --> Capture Funds from PayPal`: After obtaining PayPal authorization, the funds are captured.
- `Capture Funds from CC --> Payment Confirmation Received`: After capturing funds from credit card, payment confirmation is received.
- `Capture Funds from PayPal --> Payment Confirmation Received`: After capturing funds from PayPal, payment confirmation is received.
- `Payment Confirmation Received --> Update Order Status: Paid`: After receiving payment confirmation, the order status is updated to "Paid."
- `Update Order Status: Paid --> Inventory Management: Deduct Stock`: After updating the order status, the inventory is deducted.
- `Inventory Management: Deduct Stock --> Generate Shipping Label`: After deducting the inventory, a shipping label is generated.
- `Generate Shipping Label --> Notify Warehouse for Fulfillment`: After generating the shipping label, the warehouse is notified for fulfillment.
- `Notify Warehouse for Fulfillment --> Order Fulfilled & Shipped`: After notifying the warehouse, the order is fulfilled and shipped.
- `Order Fulfilled & Shipped --> Send Order Confirmation Email`: After fulfilling and shipping the order, an order confirmation email is sent.
- `Send Order Confirmation Email --> Log Order & Transaction Data`: After sending the confirmation email, the order and transaction data are logged.
- `Display Payment Error --> Display Generic Payment Failure`: If a payment error occurs, a generic payment failure message is displayed.
- `Display Generic Payment Failure --> User Action: Initiate Order`: After displaying the generic payment failure, the process loops back to the beginning.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Payment Gateway**: A service that authorizes credit card or direct payments processing for e-businesses, online retailers, or traditional brick and mortar.
- **Order Instance**: A representation of a specific order in the system, containing details such as customer information, ordered items, and payment details.
- **Inventory Management**: The process of tracking and managing the quantity of products in stock.
- **Shipping Label**: A label containing information required for shipping a package, such as the recipient's address and tracking number.
- **Order Fulfillment**: The process of receiving, processing, and delivering orders to customers.
- **Transaction Data**: Data related to a financial transaction, such as the amount, date, and time.
- **Authorization**: The process of verifying that a customer has sufficient funds or credit to complete a transaction.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The order processing workflow begins with the "User Action: Initiate Order," which triggers a "Product Availability Check." If the product is "Available," the flow proceeds to "Create Order Instance," "Apply Promotions/Discounts," and "Calculate Order Total." If the product is "Unavailable," a "Display Out-of-Stock Message" is shown, and the process may terminate or redirect. After calculating the total, the system prompts the user to "Select Payment Method," branching to either "CC Validation & Auth via Payment Gateway" for credit cards or "Redirect to PayPal for Login" for PayPal. Credit card validation can result in "Approved," leading to "Capture Funds from CC," or "Declined," leading to "Display Payment Error." Similarly, PayPal authorization leads to "PayPal Authorization Obtained" and "Capture Funds from PayPal." A payment gateway handles the secure transmission of credit card information during the validation process. Both payment methods converge at "Payment Confirmation Received," followed by "Update Order Status: Paid," "Inventory Management: Deduct Stock," "Generate Shipping Label," "Notify Warehouse for Fulfillment," and "Order Fulfilled & Shipped." Finally, "Send Order Confirmation Email" and "Log Order & Transaction Data" complete the process. A "Display Generic Payment Failure" node is reached from "Display Payment Error" and loops back to "User Action: Initiate Order."
