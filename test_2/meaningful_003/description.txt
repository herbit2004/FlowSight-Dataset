## Repository Context & Terminology
- **Order Request**: A request placed by a customer to purchase goods or services.
- **Inventory Availability**: The state of having sufficient stock to fulfill an order.
- **Warehouse-Service**: A service responsible for managing and tracking inventory levels.
- **Deduct Inventory**: The process of reducing the inventory count after an order is placed.
- **Deduction Successful**: The state of successfully reducing the inventory count.
- **Inventory-Txn-Manager**: A component responsible for managing transactions related to inventory changes.
- **Commit Transaction**: The process of permanently saving the changes made during a transaction.
- **Compensation**: A mechanism to undo changes made during a failed transaction, ensuring data consistency.
- **Rollback**: The process of reverting the system to its previous state.
