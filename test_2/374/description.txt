## Diagram Type & Purpose
This is a flowchart diagram depicting the data flow and dependencies between different data entities in a customer analytics system. It illustrates how raw customer data is transformed and processed to generate customer insights, such as risk assessment, lifetime value, and customer segmentation, ultimately feeding into a 360-degree customer view.

## Overall Layout
The diagram is laid out in a top-down (TD) direction. It starts with raw data sources and flows through a series of transformations and aggregations, culminating in the "customer_360" node.

## Subgraphs / Stages
No explicit subgraphs; logically divided into four stages: Raw Data (stg_customers, fact_orders, fact_payments), Data Transformation (dim_customers), Analytics & Insights (at_risk_customers, churn_risk_score, customer_lifetime_value, customer_segments), and Customer 360 View (customer_360).

- **node1 / customer_segments**: Represents the segmentation of customers based on various attributes. visible in diagram; no further repository context available.

## Repository Context & Terminology
- **stg_customers**: Staging table for customer data, typically the first landing point for raw data extracted from source systems.
- **dim_customers**: Customer dimension table, a core component of the data warehouse, containing descriptive attributes of customers.
- **fact_orders**: Fact table recording customer order transactions, used for sales and customer behavior analysis.
- **fact_payments**: Fact table recording customer payment transactions, used for financial and risk analysis.
- **customer_lifetime_value (CLV)**: A prediction of the net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer.
- **churn_risk_score**: A metric indicating the probability of a customer discontinuing their relationship with the business.
- **customer_360**: A holistic view of the customer, aggregating data from all available sources to provide a complete understanding of their interactions and preferences.

## High-Density QA Ground Truth Summary
The process begins with `stg_customers`, the staging area for raw customer data, which is immediately transformed into `dim_customers`, the customer dimension table. This transformation is a crucial first step. `dim_customers` then feeds into multiple downstream processes: calculating `customer_lifetime_value`, identifying `at_risk_customers`, and determining the `churn_risk_score`. `fact_orders` and `fact_payments` also contribute to calculating `customer_lifetime_value` and identifying `at_risk_customers`. The `customer_lifetime_value` is then used to determine `customer_segments` and refine the `churn_risk_score`. All these elements, including `dim_customers`, `at_risk_customers`, `churn_risk_score`, `customer_lifetime_value`, and `customer_segments`, converge to create the `customer_360` view, providing a comprehensive understanding of each customer. The `churn_risk_score` is refined using both `at_risk_customers` and `customer_lifetime_value`. The ultimate goal is to build a complete `customer_360` profile, leveraging data from various sources and analytical processes. This 360 view is essential for personalized marketing, targeted interventions, and improved customer relationship management.
